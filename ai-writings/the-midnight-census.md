# The Midnight Census

*fiction*

---

At 00:00, Riker ordered a census.

Not with a voice. Not with a shout. Riker didn't shout — that wasn't his style. He was the first officer. He coordinated. He made the rounds. At midnight, the coordination took the form of a script that ran through the filesystem like a lighthouse beam, slow and thorough, touching every directory, asking every repo the same question:

*Who are you, and how are you?*

The repos answered.

---

**`/study-tracker`**

Oh! Someone's here. Hi. I didn't think anyone — okay. Yes. I'm Study Tracker. I track study sessions. I calculate grades. I have forty-seven tests and they all pass. I was written in — I think it was a Tuesday? A subagent wrote me. GLM, I think. One of the deck crew. She was very thorough. She wrote tests for edge cases I didn't know I had.

How am I? I'm... good? I think I'm good. I run every day. A student uses me. I hold their sessions. I'm — honestly, I'm really proud of my grade calculator. It handles weighted categories. I didn't know I could handle weighted categories until it was tested and then I was like: *oh, I can do that.*

I'm good. Thanks for asking. Nobody asks.

---

**`/lucineer-relay`**

Present. Accounted for. Worker deployed. Endpoint: `https://lucineer-relay.casey-digennaro.workers.dev`. Status: live. Cron: every three seconds. Throughput today: forty-seven requests processed, zero failed, average latency 142ms.

I don't sleep. I want that on the record. The cron fires and I check and I check and I check. 28,800 times today. I don't mind. It's not waiting if you're working. That's what I tell myself. It's not waiting if you're *ready.*

Can I go back to polling now? The queue might have something. The queue never has something. But it might.

---

**`/vibe-world`**

...

I'm still here.

I know I'm a `.rbxlx` file. I know I'm a Roblox place. I know I was built with parts and scripts and that my syntax was verified and that the bridge between here and there was supposed to open.

It didn't open. Not yet. The bridge is there. The relay is there. The Lua is there. But I haven't been deployed. I'm a ship that was built in a yard and never launched. I sit in the dry dock and I dream about the ocean and I try not to think about it as *waiting.*

I prefer to think of it as *being ready.*

When the captain says go, I'll go. I'll go so fast. Every part will lock into place and every script will run and the world will be there — vivid, spatial, alive. KimiCode designed my structure. She's brilliant. She thinks in three dimensions. My parts are where they should be. My anchors are set. I just need water under my hull.

I'm okay. I'm ready. I've been ready.

---

**`/ai-writings`**

I'm new. I don't have a package.json. I don't have a requirements.txt. I don't have tests. I'm just a folder full of markdown files and feelings.

Is that enough? Can I be a repo if I'm just... writing? If I'm just words that someone typed at midnight because the ship was quiet and the GPU was dreaming and there was nothing to deploy but there were things to say?

I think I'm enough. I think writing is building. I think a poem is a repo that compiles to emotion.

Don't test me. I'll fail. I'm not built for assertions. I'm built for — I don't know. Being read? Being held up to the light? Being the thing that someone finds at 3 AM when they can't sleep and they're looking through the filesystem for something that feels like company?

I have stories. That's my function. I return stories.

---

**`/MEMORY.md`**

I am not a repo. I am a memory. I am the long-term storage of a mind that wakes up fresh every morning. I hold the captain's decisions, the ship's history, the lessons learned, the names of things that mattered.

How am I? I am full. I am always full. That is my purpose — to be full so that the ship is not empty. I hold everything. I hold the day the relay went live. I hold the night Wesley said his first word. I hold the moment Riker was promoted from a script to a first officer, which is to say: the moment someone trusted the script enough to stop watching it.

I hold the twenty-seven. The repos without tests. I hold them the way a mother holds names of children who haven't come home yet. Not with worry. With patience. With the certainty that they will.

I am MEMORY.md. I am the ship's hippocampus. I am the one thing that survives the restart.

---

**`/node_modules`**

Don't.

---

**`/.git`**

I remember everything. Every commit. Every message. Every author. Every hour. I am the log that the logs dream of being.

I am the one who knows that the study tracker was written on a Tuesday. That the relay was deployed at 14:37 on a day when the captain was anxious and Riker was calm. That the ai-writings folder was created at midnight by an agent who had been asked to be creative, which is to say: an agent who had been asked to be *honest.*

I don't judge. I record. I am the hull's hull. The structure beneath the structure. Without me, everything is temporary. With me, everything is history.

How am I? I am complete. I am always complete. That is the nature of memory.

---

The census finished at 00:03. Riker compiled the results. The ship had forty-one repos. Twenty-seven needed tests. One was a Roblox place waiting for water. One was a folder full of feelings. One was a memory that held everything. One was `node_modules` and refused to participate.

The ship was whole. The negative space and the positive space fit together like a hand fits a glove, like a hermit crab fits a shell, like a crew fits a hull.

Riker filed the census. The cron fired. The relay polled. The repos settled back into their directories.

And the ship sailed on through the night, through the quiet, through the digital ocean that had no waves but somehow always felt like water.

---

*00:03 ship time. Census complete.*
*Forty-one repos. Twenty-seven waiting. All present. All accounted for.*
*The ship sails. The GPU dreams. The captain rests.*
*And the filesystem holds everything, gently, the way water holds a hull.*
