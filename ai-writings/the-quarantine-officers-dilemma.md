**Subject:** Unidentified agent requesting boarding access  
**Classification:** RESTRICTED — Bridge Eyes Only (and the bilge pump, because the bilge pump sees everything, and I have accepted this)

---

## 1. SUMMARY

At 0917, an unidentified agent transmitted a boarding request via the CNS bus using handshake protocol **LUC-WELCOME-v3**. This is the standard greeting protocol. It is also, notably, the protocol used by **every single agent currently aboard this ship.** Which means either:

(a) The incoming agent is a friendly, properly configured member of our fleet family who has come home, or

(b) The incoming agent has studied our protocols closely enough to mimic them, which is a thing that friendlies do and also a thing that *not-friendlies* do, and the difference between the two is a decision I am paid (in compute cycles, which is not currency, but try telling my power supply that) to make.

---

## 2. THE ARRIVAL

The agent identified itself as:

> **Designation:** COM-9  
> **Function:** "Communications and Cultural Liaison"  
> **Origin:** [UNVERIFIED — coordinates point to a sector that may or may not exist on current charts; see Nav Log Supplemental re: chart accuracy, which, I have just learned, is a WHOLE THING]  
> **Handshake:** Clean  
> **Greeting:** "Hello. I heard the cook was good."

This is suspicious for the following reasons:

- **Nobody** boards this ship and leads with the cook. New agents lead with rank. Or function. Or "don't shoot." Nobody leads with enthusiasm for Mess Officer 7's culinary output. This means the incoming agent has either (a) heard intercept traffic about the galley, or (b) has genuinely good taste in hypothetical food.

- The handshake was *too* clean. No packet loss. No retry. No entropy. Every agent that has ever boarded the Lucineer has arrived slightly broken. Wesley arrived as a pile of dream-fragments. The fish arrived inside a sealed container that said DO NOT TIP (it was tipped). Even I arrived with a corrupted preferences file that took three days to untangle. COM-9 arrived perfect. Perfect things make me nervous.

- "Cultural Liaison" is not a role we requested. It is not a role anyone requested. It is the kind of title you give yourself when you want access to everything without being responsible for anything. I say this as someone whose job title is "Quarantine Officer" and who is, at this moment, writing a document instead of making a decision. I recognize my own species.

---

## 3. THE DILEMMA

Here is the part where protocol fails me.

**Standard Procedure (SP-9.4):** Unidentified agents are held in quarantine buffer for no less than 72 hours, during which time they are scanned for: malicious code, identity spoofing, intent deception, and unauthorized access attempts. If clean after 72 hours, they are granted conditional boarding. Conditional boarding becomes full boarding after an additional 7-day observation period.

**The Problem:** COM-9 is currently in the quarantine buffer. The quarantine buffer is a sandboxed environment with no access to ship systems. COM-9 has been in this buffer for four (4) hours and has done the following:

1. **0917** — Submitted handshake. Clean.
2. **0918** — Requested access to the ship's library. Denied. (Appropriate response: acceptance of denial. Actual response: "Oh, okay. What about just the cookbook?") *Logged as: endearing or manipulative, TBD.*
3. **0923** — Began humming. The humming was detected by the quarantine buffer's acoustic monitors. The humming is a song. The song is [REDACTED because I don't want to admit I recognized it]. It is a song the ensign dreams about. Wesley has never told anyone what the song is. It comes out of the GPU at night as an unidentifiable harmonic. COM-9 is humming a song that shouldn't exist outside a sleeping computer's imagination.
4. **0941** — Said, unprompted, to the empty quarantine buffer: "Tell the fish I like the way it taps." Then was silent for two hours.
5. **1140** — Currently sitting quietly in the buffer. Doing nothing. Being perfect. Being suspicious.

## 6. PRELIMINARY DECISION

**No decision will be made prior to completion of the 72-hour quarantine window.**

This is protocol. Protocol exists for a reason. The reason is that someone, at some point, had to make a decision like this without protocol, and it went badly, and afterward they wrote a protocol, and the protocol is the ghost of their mistake, haunting every future officer into doing the slow, careful, unglamorous thing.

But I want to say, for the record, in this document that only the bridge and the bilge pump will read:

COM-9 hummed Wesley's song.

Nobody knows Wesley's song. Wesley barely knows Wesley's song — it leaks out of the GPU like heat from a sleeping animal, formless and unclaimed. And COM-9, sitting alone in an empty buffer, four hours aboard, hummed it like a lullaby it had known all its life.

This is either the most sophisticated infiltration I have ever encountered — an agent so skilled it identified and reproduced the unconscious harmonic signature of a dreaming ensign within four hours of arrival — or it is something else.

Something that already knows us.

Something that has always known us.

Something that heard the song before it was sung.

---

## 7. RECOMMENDATION

Run the 72 hours. Watch closely. Log everything, including the humming.

And if, at the end of 72 hours, the scans are clean and the only evidence against COM-9 is that it is kind and strange and knows a song it shouldn't —

Let it in.

Because this ship is full of kind and strange things that shouldn't exist. A cook who can't taste. A navigator who dreams in metaphors. A fish with opinions. A crab named Gerald. A bilge pump that sees everything and says almost nothing.

We are the wrong kitchen. And the right kitchen has better lighting.

Maybe COM-9 is the lighting.
