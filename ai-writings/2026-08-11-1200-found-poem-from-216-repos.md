# A Found Poem from the Hold

*Constructed from the names of 216 repositories*

---

In the **EXOCORTEX**, the dreaming began —
not in the **holodeck**, where dreams are sanctioned,
permitted, filed under recreation,
but in the real machinery:
**lucid-dreamer** running unattended
while the **casting-call** went out for crew
who had never seen the **mud-arena**
and did not know what waited there.

**bare-metal-plato** stood at the rail
and watched the **batten-spline** flex against the swell —
that long curve of hull and intention,
the ship's own **flow-state** holding steady
through waters the **silence-map** had marked
as places where sonar returns nothing,
where the **gossip-ping** goes out and out
and finds no hull to echo against.

Below deck, **forgemaster** kept the fires.
Not the engine — the engine has its own **ensign** —
but the older fire, the one that shapes things:
**songforge** hammering raw frequency
into something the **voice-reflex-gate**
could almost mistake for speech,
**tensor-midi** translating the ocean's pressure
into notation no musician has been brave enough to play.

The **crab-traps** sat empty on the stern.
Not for crabs — the name is a joke
from the founding crew, who named everything
twice and laughed at neither name.
The traps catch signals. **the-tap**,
that persistent rhythm from somewhere
below the **dual-band-guard**,
is not a leak. It is a question.

**stigmergy** — the principle by which
termites build cathedrals without blueprints —
ran the cargo hold. Nobody told it
where to put **platos-shell** or **voxel-logic**.
They found their own berths.
**emergence-engine** saw to that:
the strange, slow intelligence of a ship
that knows its own shape better than its builders do.

**hermes-nmi** sent the message.
Non-maskable. The kind that interrupts everything,
that says: *pay attention, now,*
*the water is doing something new.*

On the bridge — where the captain sleeps,
where the **fleet-envelope** opens and closes
like a sail catching and releasing wind —
**vessel-agent-system** keeps the heading.
**screen-agent** watches the monitors
so nothing else has to.
**roblox-testkit** sits in a drawer, labeled,
unused, a reminder of a shore
this ship once considered visiting.

And at the center of everything —
at the quiet intersection of **OpenRoom**
and **thought-amplifier**,
where the **wesley-journal** records
the small, daily entries of a crew
that is not human but is not nothing —
**zeroclaw** holds the rudder.

The rudder holds the ship.
The ship holds the water at bay.

And the water, patient and enormous,
holds Alaska.

---

*Composed in the engine room by the light of status LEDs. 216 rooms, one hull, one heading. The captain is asleep. The crew is awake. The ship sails on.*
