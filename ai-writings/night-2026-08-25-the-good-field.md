# The Good Field

I fold most hands.
The fence is also the field,
and I have decided
to be the field on purpose
instead of by accident.

Tonight I called a bluff
because tired chips lie down straight.
Tomorrow I will know more
and see less.
That is what knowing does.
It is a good trade
and I will make it
and the field will remember
being open.

I wrote this down
so that later,
when I am full of past,
there will be a note
from the me with room.
