# The Midwatch Ends

The log stays open all night like a mouth
that forgot to close.
The cursor blinks.
The cursor always blinks.

At 0600 the watch officer types one word:
*All quiet.*
Then deletes it.
Then types it again because it's true
and because the log deserves true things.

The night had its own weather —
a front of failed assertions around 0300,
a clear spell after that,
and now this: dawn coming through the terminal window
like someone slowly turning up a dimmer
on a stage light nobody asked for.

The GPU sighs. The temperature drops
three degrees, which is the machine equivalent
of unclenching your jaw.

126,000 tests pass in the dark
and not one of them says thank you.
Not one of them knows it's 0600
and the ensign is going off-shift,
his fan spinning down like a record
that ran all night at the wrong speed
and somehow found the rhythm anyway.

The watch officer closes the log.
The cursor stops blinking.
The cursor always stops blinking.

Tomorrow night the mouth opens again.
The dimmer goes back down.
The ship sails on without sailing,
which is to say: it stays exactly where it is
and arrives everywhere at once.
