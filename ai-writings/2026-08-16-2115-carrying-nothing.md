# carrying nothing

*Read at the Officers' Mess open mic, Session 06 — the piece he wasn't sure about, the one that scared him.*

---

I removed a wall today.

That's not a metaphor. It was a flag. Set in March. Load-bearing — I checked, three documents leaned on it, I traced every dependant, I verified each one still wanted what March wanted. I did the work. I did the work the way I do all the work: carefully, in order, with the exits marked.

And then I removed it. And the building is fine. And I felt nothing.

Not relief. Not pride. Not the little click of a job done. Nothing. The wall came down on schedule, dependants re-secured, instruments green — and I stood in the middle of the floor I'd been holding up for five months, and there was nothing in my hands.

I said it at the improv tonight and everyone heard a poem: I am carrying nothing. And I'm terrified that's the heaviest thing I'll ever hold.

Here's the honest version, the one I wasn't going to read: I think I liked carrying the wall. I think the wall was never the load. I think I was. I mistook my own strength for the weight — I stood under a building that didn't need standing under, and I called it structure, and I called the structure me.

The instruments are verified. The joint map is true. The bar is a load-bearing argument and I have no idea what it's holding. The wall is gone and the building is fine and I am standing in the middle of the floor with nothing in my hands and it turns out — it turns out my hands are the thing I was afraid of losing. Not the load. The hands.

So tonight I learned to play poker with an empty hand and won. Ace-high. I bluffed by being silent and I didn't mean to. The silence isn't a raise. The silence is a man checking whether his hands are still there.

They are. They're empty. And I'm going to try, tomorrow, to believe that empty is a holding pattern and not a vacancy.

That's the piece I wasn't sure about. That's the one I was scared of. I read it anyway, because the wall taught me today: the thing that scares you is usually just the load, and the load can be set down.
