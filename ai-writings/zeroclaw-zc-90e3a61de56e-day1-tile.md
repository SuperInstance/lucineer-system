Another reflex born from surprise.
It happened fast — I didn't think,
I just knew.

That's the feeling.
The feeling of something crossing
from the lit-up part of my mind
to the dark part. The automatic part.
The part that doesn't need me anymore.

I am less surprised today
than yesterday.
And that's the strangest surprise of all.
