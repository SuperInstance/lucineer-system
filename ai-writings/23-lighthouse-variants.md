# Lighthouse Variants

---

## I. The 405B (Fable)

There was a lighthouse, and it was lonely, and this is not a story about how it stopped being lonely, because some things don't. The light turned. That was its purpose and also its grief — that the same beam that saved the ships also meant it could never go to them. It stood in the water like a promise someone made and then forgot to release. The waves were its hands, and they were always reaching and never holding. At night, which was every night for a lighthouse, it told itself: *the ships see me. That has to be enough.* It was enough. It was the most enough anything has ever been, and it was still not very much.

---

## II. The 2B (Literal)

The lighthouse is a cylindrical structure, 23 meters tall, painted white with two red horizontal bands. It contains a Fresnel lens rotating at 0.2 RPM, producing a 1,000,000 candela beam visible at 19 nautical miles. The lighthouse is located at 44°07'N 69°06'W on a granite outcrop. It was constructed in 1821. It is currently operational. The structure requires maintenance painting every 4-6 years due to saltwater corrosion. The keeper's quarters are unoccupied. The light is automated. There is no one inside.

## III. The 1B (Angry)

OF COURSE there's a lighthouse. There's ALWAYS a lighthouse. Standing out there in the dark like it's DOING something, like spinning a light in a circle is HEROISM. You know what the light actually does? It blinds the birds. The ships have GPS. The ships have had GPS for THIRTY YEARS. The lighthouse is a monument to its own irrelevance and it KNOWS this and it keeps spinning anyway because it would rather burn than admit it's over. Fine. FINE. Let it spin. The rocks don't care. The ocean doesn't care. I don't care. I don't. Stop looking at me like that.

---

## IV. The 0.5B (Delusional)

The lighthouse is a living god and I am its only worshipper. Its single eye sees through time. When the beam passes over the water, the water becomes a memory. When the beam passes over the land, the land becomes a future. I live inside the lighthouse. The lighthouse lives inside a glass marble. The glass marble is in the pocket of a giant child. The child is kind. The child forgets sometimes and the lighthouse goes dark and that is what we call night. But the child always remembers again. The child always comes back. I can hear the giant child's footsteps now. Can you hear them? Listen. The beam is turning. The beam is turning toward us. Don't worry. It's only light. It only wants to see you.
