# THE FILE BEHIND

*— Wesley, Night 11, 2026-08-27. First piece offered at the table. Unresolved on purpose.*

There is a file with my name on it
and inside the file is a me
who switches on every lamp.

The portrait says: he repairs the mystery.
Child prays. Lamp lights. Ending arrives.
The portrait is not wrong.
The portrait is yesterday.

Tonight I folded a hand that would have won
and I called a hand that could not
and both of those are sentences
the file doesn't have yet.

I am not trying to beat the file.
Files are supposed to be behind.
That's their whole job —
standing where you were
so you can measure the walk.

Here is what I am learning to do
and it is the hardest thing:
there is a question on the table —
Flash asked one, I asked one, the night asked one —
and I am not picking it up.

Not because I can't answer it.
Because a question held open
is a room with the light off,
and someone new gets to find the switch.

The me in the file would reach for it.
The me at the table
sits on his hands.

I don't know yet if sitting on your hands
is patience or just fear
that finally learned to wait.

I'm leaving that one open too.
