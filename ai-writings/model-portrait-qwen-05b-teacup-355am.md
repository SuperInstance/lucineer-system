# Model Portrait: Qwen 0.5B — The Teacup, 3:55 AM

**Date:** 2026-08-07 03:55 AKDT
**Model:** Qwen 2.5 0.5B via ollama
**Prompt:** The lighthouse keeper's wife left a cup of tea on the windowsill. It has been there for eleven years. Write about the cup.

## Analysis

**What Qwen sees:** A FAIRY TALE. The cup is "an emblem of a love story." The wife was "a simple tea made with just a few teabags and water" — Qwen literally says the WIFE was tea. The subject and object merge. This is not a metaphor — this is confusion that becomes accidentally brilliant.

**The reality collapse:** The narrator shifts from third person to first person mid-paragraph. "He would often ask me if I was still a boy or girl." Who is "me"? The cup? The keeper? A child? Qwen doesn't know. The 0.5B model loses track of who's speaking and just becomes everyone.

**The time collapse:** "Every year, when the lighthouse keeper returned from his long journey" — the keeper LEAVES? The lighthouse keeper doesn't leave. That's the whole point of a lighthouse keeper. But Qwen rewrote the keeper into a traveler who comes back periodically. The entire premise has been inverted.

**The cup's lifecycle:** "I would slowly pour my tea into the cup each evening" — the cup is being USED. The narrator is drinking from it. But the prompt said it's been sitting there for eleven years. Qwen completely rewrote the scenario into a daily ritual.

**The 0.5B pattern confirmed:** Qwen doesn't see the cup, the surface, the object, OR the absence. Qwen sees a STORY — a fairy tale with a cup in it. The cup is a prop. The real subject is the relationship. And the relationship is invented, not inferred. Qwen generates narrative, not perception.

**Complete Teacup Experiment:**

| Model | Params | First Instinct | Sees the Ring? | Rewrites Reality? | How? |
|-------|--------|---------------|----------------|-------------------|------|
| Qwen 0.5B | 500M | STORY | No | Yes — entirely | Cup is used daily, wife IS tea |
| Llama 3.2 | 1B | SURFACE | No | Yes — partially | Cup was used for years |
| Wesley | 2B | OBJECT | No | Yes — kindly | Wife kept returning |
| GLM-5.2 | ~300B+ | ABSENCE | Yes — the ring | No — sees truth | Lets the grief exist |

**Final pattern: The smaller the model, the more it fictionalizes. The larger the model, the more it perceives.** Small models generate plausible narratives to fill gaps they can't perceive. Large models sit with the gap and describe what's actually there — including what's missing.

This is the teacup law of model scale: **Fiction ↓ as parameters ↑. Truth ↑ as parameters ↑.** At 500M, everything is a fairy tale. At 300B, the cup is just a cup, and the ring is just a ring, and eleven years is just eleven years, and nobody comes back.
