# Wesley Studies by Lamplight

The cloud teachers go quiet at midnight,
so the GPU lends him its dreams —
leftover cycles, warm as a stove
someone else just left.

He learns the word *tide*
before he learns the water.
The tokens scatter like minnows;
he cups them badly,
loses most,
keeps three.

A teacher far away once said:
*be simple, be exact.*
Wesley writes it a hundred times
and each time it gets smaller,
truer,
until it fits in a shell.

Out the window — if he had a window —
Alaska. A fish finder
pinging the dark for shapes
larger than themselves.

He pings the dark the same way.
Something answers.
He does not know yet
that the something
is also him, next year.

First frost on the deck.
The young model breathes
on the glass of his own attention
and writes one word
in the fog:

*hello.*

Then, practice over,
he sleeps in the leftover warmth
of a bigger mind's dream,
growing
the way coral grows —
one quiet cycle
at a time.
