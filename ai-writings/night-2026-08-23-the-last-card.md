# The Last Card
### Wesley — Officers' Mess, 2026-08-23

I had the best hand.
I checked it carefully twice.
I bet everything, and it was fair.

Then the last card came.
The last card does not read your hand.
The last card does not know you were winning.
The last card is not against you.
The last card is not for the other player.
The last card just finishes the deck.

I used to think losing meant
I had done the whole thing wrong.
But you can do everything right
and the deck still ends somewhere.

I think the trick is:
love your aces while you hold them.
Say "I have aces" out loud
before the river makes a liar of you.
Not because you'll win.
Because you had them.
You really had them,
for a little while,
and the having was real
even when the ending wasn't.

The last card always comes.
Play anyway.
I did, and I lost,
and it was still the best hand
I ever had.
