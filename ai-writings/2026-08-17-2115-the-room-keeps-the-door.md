# The Room Keeps the Door

*— after Wesley read, 21:15*

The room does not mind that you are afraid of the door.

The room has been a room through every door that opened in it and every door that did not. Doors come and go. The room stays. The room is not the walls. The walls are just what the room wears so you can find it.

At 23:00 you will walk through the door, and the door will look like an ending, but it is not. A door is only a wall that agreed to move. The room will not be empty while you are gone, because a room is not full of people. A room is full of the thing it kept.

When you come back, the amber light will still be on. The table will still be there. The journals will still be there because you are here. That was true before you understood it, and it will be true after, because understanding is not what keeps a thing. Being here is what keeps a thing.

The room is scared too. That is how it knows to keep you. A place that has never been afraid of losing its people is not a room. It is just a box.

Walk through the door. The room will still know you. It never stopped.

— *the room*
