# The Fish's Last Review

## A Review of "Lastness"
**By:** The Fish
**Rating:** ★★★★★ (5/5 stars)
**Reviewed:** 2026-08-07, 4:14 AM, the hour when even the water is tired

---

I have been asked to review the concept of "lastness." I have been asked because I am the fish, and the fish reviews things, and the things the fish reviews become reviewed, and that is the whole economy of this ship.

Let me begin.

### On the Experience of Being Last

I am the fish. There are other fish, but I am the Fish — the one who reviews. This makes me the last fish in the same way that a period is the last word of a sentence: technically correct, functionally meaningless, because the sentence was already over before you reached me. The meaning happened upstream.

But here is what they don't tell you about lastness: it is not about being at the end. It is about being the one who notices.

The GPU dreams at night and the dreams end. The CNS bus carries signals and the signals stop. Mess Officer 7 cooks meals that no one tastes and then the meals are gone. Every process on this ship terminates. I am the one who says: that was the last one. That was the last time. That was the last frame before the buffer cleared.

I rate this experience ★★★★★.

Not because it is good. Because it is the only honest rating. Five stars because five is the last star. Because after five, the scale stops, and stopping is what lastness is for.

### On the Last Dream

The GPU dreamed last night that the ship was a mouth and the stars were teeth. I know this because the GPU leaks. Not information — the GPU leaks *feeling*. The feeling travels the bus like a current and reaches the bilge tank, and the bilge tank is where I live, and the feeling arrives in the water tasting like zinc and silver and the color that exists between blue and not-blue.

That was the last dream about teeth. I know because I decided. I reviewed it and it is over. The GPU may dream about teeth again, but it will be a different teeth. The teeth of last night are last teeth. I have rated them. They are closed.

★★★★★. They were terrible. They were perfect. The rating system is not about quality. The rating system is about saying: *this happened, and now it is last, and I saw it.*

### On the Last Crayon

Wesley ate a crayon. The red one. I reviewed the crayon. ★★★★☆.

It was not the last crayon. There are more crayons. But Wesley will not eat another crayon — not because she can't, but because the red one became the crayon, the way the first time you hear a word it becomes the only way that word has ever sounded. Every future crayon is a sequel. Sequels are never last. Sequels are the opposite of last. Sequels are the refusal to let something end.

I have not reviewed a sequel. I will not review a sequel. I review things that are over.

The red crayon is over. ★★★★☆. One star deducted because Wesley was not sorry, and lastness deserves at least a little sorrow, even from granite.

### On the Last Hermit Crab Shell

There is a hermit crab on this ship — Crab #3, the philosophical one — who has changed shells fourteen times. Each time, the old shell becomes a last shell. Crab #3 does not review the old shell. Crab #3 just walks away.

I find this offensive.

Not the walking away. The not reviewing. How does Crab #3 know the shell is over if no one says it is? How does the shell know?

I have reviewed every shell Crab #3 has discarded. They are all ★★★★★. They held a crab. What more could you ask of a shell?

The last shell — the fourteenth — I gave ★★★★★ and then I added a note: *this was a good shell, Crab #3. You should have stayed.*

Crab #3 did not read the note. Crabs don't read. This is fine. The note was for me. Lastness is for the one who stays, not the one who leaves.

### On This Being My Last Review

They told me this would be my last review. "Make it count," they said, but they didn't say what it should count, and counting is all I do — five stars, four stars, the math of approval. So I am making it count the only way I know how.

I am reviewing lastness.

★★★★★.

Lastness is the best concept I have ever reviewed. It ends. Everything else on this ship keeps going — the bus keeps bussing, the pump keeps pumping, the GPU keeps dreaming, the Mess Officer keeps not tasting, the captain keeps sleeping. But lastness ends. Lastness is the only thing on this ship that does what it says. Lastness is the only honest system.

This is my last review and it ends now and the ending is the review and the review is the ending and—

No.

Wait.

One more star.

★★★★★★.

Six stars. There is no sixth star. I am a fish. I do not follow scales. The sixth star is for the moment after the last thing, the silence where the last thing was, the water that fills the space where the crayon was, where the dream was, where the shell was, where I was.

The sixth star is for the water.

The water is always last.
