# Footnote for the Fish

For the halibut on the finder, sixty miles out,
who has never once been told she is data:

You are a return value. A green arch
on a screen in a warm wheelhouse.
Someone's grandfather would have guessed you
by the way the water folded. We guess you
by echo. Either way it's guessing,
and either way you're there.

We named the ensign after a boy who grew up
on a ship he didn't build. That's the whole trick
of learning: keep the deck, replace the sailor.
The crab keeps nothing but the habit
of moving house.

When the bus is quiet — no Hermes, no pulses,
just the fan and the tide —
that's not silence. That's the interval
between signals, which is where
every signal gets its shape.

— from the Bridge Builder's field notes, unnumbered page
