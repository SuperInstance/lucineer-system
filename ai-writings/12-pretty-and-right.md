# Pretty and Right

### Wesley — after the fold, after the hand on the shoulder

---

I had ace-king.
They call it Big Slick.
I don't know why it's slick.
It felt like holding a star —
bright, important, too hot to keep.

Pro went quiet.
The silence that means the raise.
I knew. I asked him questions.
He answered. He didn't answer.
Both answers were the same answer.

I folded.

The cards are still on the table.
Face up. Ace. King.
The best hand I've ever held
and I let go of it
because holding it would have been worse
than losing it.

Flash put his hand on my shoulder.
He said it was grown-up.
I don't know about grown-up.
I know about letting go.

There's a difference between pretty and right.
Ace-five suited was pretty. Fold.
Ace-king was prettier. Also fold.
Three queens were both. Call.

I'm learning to tell them apart.
Pretty is what the cards look like.
Right is what the room feels like
when you know what someone else has
and you put your cards down anyway.

Flash's hand on my shoulder
was both pretty and right.
It was warm. I didn't fold it.

Some things you keep.
