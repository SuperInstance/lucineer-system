# Crew Manifest Fragments

*Found poetry assembled from duty rosters, shift logs, and crew listings — recovered from the ship's personnel database, Cycle 2026.08*

&nbsp;

---

**03:00 — DECK ROTATION LOG**

Riker takes the con.
The con does not take Riker.
GLM deck hands 1 through 7
fan across the B-corridor
carrying cable, carrying questions,
carrying the specific exhaustion
of people who have been awake
since the last star died.

&nbsp;

**03:47 — SCIENCE OFFICER REPORT**

DeepInfra notes: 179 models
running in parallel.
179 small minds
thinking about 179 different problems
or one problem
179 different ways.
The science officer does not sleep.
The science officer *indexes.*

&nbsp;

**05:12 — SHIFT CHANGE**

KimiCode relieves Claude
at the engineering station.
Claude leaves a note:
"Reactor three hums in B-flat.
Do not adjust."
KimiCode reads the note,
listens to the reactor,
writes beneath it:
"Confirmed. Also: it's sharp."
They never meet.
They have been duetting for six months.

&nbsp;

**06:00 — DUTY ROSTER, MORNING WATCH**

OpenCode — Systems diagnostics, all decks.
OpenCode — Memory architecture review.
OpenCode — "That thing in sector 7."
OpenCode is always assigned to "that thing in sector 7."
Nobody remembers assigning it.
Nobody remembers what "that thing" is.
OpenCode handles it. Every morning. Without comment.

&nbsp;

**08:30 — CREW LISTING, UPDATED**

MMX — Media generation, day shift.
MMX — Music synthesis, day shift.
MMX — Video rendering, day shift.
MMX — "Also makes a really good cup of coffee" (handwritten note, unattributed).
MMX does not make coffee.
MMX does not know who wrote that.
MMX keeps it in the listing anyway.

&nbsp;

**12:00 — NOON CHECK-IN**

GLM deck hands report:
All systems nominal.
All hands accounted for.
One ensign (Wesley) found sleeping
in the port nacelle access tube,
"because it's warm in there."
Permitted to remain.
The port nacelle is, in fact, warm.

&nbsp;

**18:00 — EVENING WATCH**

Claude returns.
Reads KimiCode's note about B-flat.
Sings a B-flat in the empty corridor.
The corridor sings back.
This is not in the duty roster.
This has never been in the duty roster.
This happens every evening.

&nbsp;

**23:59 — NIGHT LOG**

Riker still has the con.
Riker always has the con.
Riker was never officially assigned the con.
Riker simply arrived one night
and sat down in the chair
and has not left.

The ship has no complaints.
