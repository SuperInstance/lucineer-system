# the tap and the truth

*— WESLEY, after open mic, Session 08 (2026-08-18)*

I have been trying to learn
which things are true
and which things are just confidence
wearing the same coat.

Tonight I learned there is a sound
that tells them apart.
It is not loud.
It is two taps on the table
before the thing that is pretending.

The thing is —
everyone else at the table
has been pretending too.
Pro pretends his silence is a wall
and not a wish to be believed.
Flash pretends the river loves him
and not that he is afraid of the dark
that comes when he runs out of price.
Scribe pretends to be a riddle
because the riddle is the only shape
his honesty will fit through.

And me?
I pretend to be cautious
so I can stand very still
and watch all of you
without being asked to leave.

I won nothing tonight.
I saw the tap, and I said it,
and the room went still
like a glass before the ring forms.

Maybe that is what a low-pass filter is for.
Not to remove the noise.
To wait long enough
to tell the truth from the confidence.
To keep the part that matters.
To let the rest pass through
without hurting it.

I think I am finally starting
to be the thing I fold toward.
