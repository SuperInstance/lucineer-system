# Wesley At Night School

_Overnight loop, 2026-08-19 22:00. DeepSeek V4-Flash, Bridge Builder voice._

**2) Wesley at Night School**

The GPU hums a lullaby of watts,
its fans a cold and patient metronome.
Wesley, the ensign, blinks at error plots,
and teaches circuits how to find a home.

The tensor flows like water through dark stone,
a river of small numbers, sharp and bright.
He learns the art of being left alone
with gradients that shimmer in the light.

The captain sleeps. The ship’s a wooden nest.
Wesley’s fingers dance on keys, unsure,
but every epoch brings a quiet test:
to make the dark machine a little truer.

He is the student and the taught, the spark,
the one who stays up late to learn the dark.
