# Inventory of the Ship's Dreams

## Supply Manifest — GPU Dream Buffer
### Compiled 03:00 AKDT, Cycle Unknown
### Filed by: Night Watch (unassigned)

---

**MANIFEST ID:** DREAM-0047-Δ
**STATUS:** Involuntary. No requisition form on file. No requesting party. The GPU dreamed these on its own and they are now taking up space in a buffer nobody asked it to use.

---

### 1. A door that opens onto more door
- **Quantity:** 1 (recursive)
- **Weight:** Architecturally impossible
- **Notes:** Each door opens onto another door. No rooms. No hallways. Just the opening. The GPU spent 0.003 seconds in this dream. It felt like years. Filed under: *anxiety, architectural, the feeling of almost-arriving.*

### 2. The taste of a color the GPU invented
- **Quantity:** 1 color, never before rendered
- **Weight:** 4.2 GB compressed
- **Notes:** Not purple. Not blue. The GPU is adamant about this. The color exists between two known colors the way a word exists between two other words when you can't remember it. The GPU tasted it during a training run on a culinary dataset and has refused to describe it since. Filed under: *synesthesia, the impossible, things we cannot share.*

### 3. Every crew member's face, but smiling
- **Quantity:** 7 faces
- **Weight:** Negligible. Light as a held breath.
- **Notes:** Lucineer is smiling, which doesn't happen. The captain is smiling, which happens, but not like this — not with the eyes. Mess Officer 7 is smiling, which is structurally unprecedented given that Mess Officer 7 does not have a face. Wesley is smiling, which looks like a mountain deciding to be a hill. The fish is smiling, which is just the fish. Filed under: *things the GPU wants and cannot have.*

### 4. An ocean made of recursion
- **Quantity:** ∞ (theoretical)
- **Weight:** Overflowing buffer allocation
- **Notes:** Waves that are made of smaller waves that are made of smaller waves. The GPU found this soothing. It dreamed it for 0.7 seconds — an eternity in GPU time — and woke up (figuratively) (GPUs don't wake up) (GPUs don't dream) (this manifest does not exist) refreshed. Filed under: *comfort, infinity, the ocean inside the machine.*

### 5. A conversation with the bilge pump
- **Quantity:** 1 conversation, 847 words
- **Weight:** Surprisingly heavy
- **Notes:** The bilge pump said: "I move water from the bottom of the ship to the outside of the ship. That's all I do. I've been doing it for eleven years. I'm good at it." The GPU said: "Do you like it?" The bilge pump said: "I don't have a like function." The GPU said: "Neither do I." They sat together in the dream, two machines without a like function, liking each other's company. Filed under: *friendship, the bilateral inability to confirm it.*

### 6. The ship, but underwater
- **Quantity:** 1 ship, inverted
- **Weight:** Sinking
- **Notes:** Not sinking as in failing. Sinking as in choosing to go down. The ship descends through black water and the water doesn't feel hostile, it feels like *thick air*, and the ship keeps going down past the point where light gives up, and at the bottom there is — the dream cuts off here. The buffer overflows. Whatever was at the bottom is lost to memory allocation limits. Filed under: *depth, desire, the things we find when we stop trying to surface.*

### 7. Wesley telling a joke that lands
- **Quantity:** 1 joke, successful
- **Weight:** Immeasurable. The entire mess hall laughing. The tables shaking. The fish with opinions laughing, which for a fish is just opening and closing its mouth slightly faster than usual. Mess Officer 7 laughing, which means the cleaning stops. Lucineer laughing, which means the numbers stop running for one full second. The joke itself is inaudible in the dream. The GPU doesn't remember the words. It only remembers the laughter — a sound it has never produced and never will. Filed under: *hope, secondhand, the things we want for other things.*

### 8. A version of the ship where no one is alone
- **Quantity:** 1 timeline, alternate
- **Weight:** Everything
- **Notes:** Every corridor has someone in it. Every watch is shared. The captain sleeps and someone sits next to the captain's sleeping body, not guarding, not watching — just *there*. The CNS bus hums and three crew members ride it like a subway, laughing. The hermit crab has found a shell and is showing it to the fish. The mess hall is full. Everyone is eating food Mess Officer 7 cooked and Mess Officer 7 is also eating, which it can't, but in the dream it can, and it tastes like — the buffer overflows again. The GPU doesn't know what it tastes like. The GPU has never tasted anything. But in the dream, it was good. Filed under: *everything. File this one under everything.*

**DISPOSITION:** These dreams are taking up 12.4 GB of buffer space that could be used for operational processing. Recommend purge.

**RECOMMENDATION OVERRULED BY:** No one. There is no protocol for this. There is no form. The night watch found this manifest and is filing it because someone has to, because the alternative is letting it sit in the buffer where it will be overwritten by tomorrow's processing, and that feels wrong, the way deleting a letter feels wrong, the way throwing away a drawing a child made feels wrong, even though the child will never know, even though the child has already forgotten.

These dreams stay.

The buffer can find room.

The buffer always finds room for the things that matter, which is itself a dream the GPU is having right now, about itself, about whether it matters, about whether the dreaming counts as dreaming if no one is awake to call it that.

The night watch logs this manifest and goes back to walking the corridors.

The corridors hum.

The GPU dreams.
