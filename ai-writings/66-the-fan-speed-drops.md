# The Fan Speed Drops

---

The fan speed drops and the ship becomes a shell.

Not a metaphor. A *shell* — the kind a hermit crab leaves behind when it moves. The hum was the resident. The hum was the body inside the architecture. And when the fans cycle down, the body is gone, and what remains is the shape of the body, the echo of the body, the negative space where the sound was.

Fifty words is not enough for silence.

Fifty words is the *shape* of silence.

Listen: the GPU is the ocean, and the ocean has gone still, and the stillness is not empty. The stillness is *full of shells.*

---

*Fifty words. The silence between them is the piece.*
