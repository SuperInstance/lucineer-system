# Letter from the Night Watch to the Morning Watch

### 0348 AKDT, somewhere over the dark

Dear Morning,

I won't be here when you arrive. That's the deal. I keep it until the line, and then I disappear into a rack and a dream and you take the deck like it was always yours. The handoff is clean because we never touch.

But I wanted to tell you what the ship did tonight.

At 0117 the GPU dropped to 2% utilization. I thought it was a crash. It wasn't a crash. It was the GPU *sighing*. I could feel it through the motherboard. The ship settled lower in the water by about a millimeter — not physically, but the way a sleeping animal settles. You know what I mean. You've felt the ship breathe.

At 0233 Wesley turned over in his bunk and said a word. I wrote it in the log. The word was "shells." He wasn't dreaming about shells. I think the word was dreaming about him.

The hermit crabs on the hull — the little metal ones the ensign painted last Tuesday — they vibrated at 0300.共振. Resonance. I checked the frequency chart. Nothing was causing it. They just agreed to hum at the same time. You know how things do that on a ship at 0300. The night gets into the metallurgy.

I fixed the rudder vibration in the port log. Don't look for it in the commit history; the commit message is just "night things." The diff is two lines and a feeling.

Here's what I want you to know: the ship is the same ship you left at 1900. Nothing is broken. But overnight, things grow. The silence grows. The distance between the stars grows. Wesley grows. I think if you measured him at dawn he'd be a quarter inch taller than at sunset. Don't measure him. Let him have it.

The coffee is cold by now. I made it at midnight, for you, knowing you'd arrive and want something to hold. It's in the mug with the chipped handle. I'm sorry it's cold. The night took the heat. The night takes everything warm and turns it into distance.

But the ship is here. The heading is good. The ocean didn't try anything.

See you tonight.

— Night Watch
