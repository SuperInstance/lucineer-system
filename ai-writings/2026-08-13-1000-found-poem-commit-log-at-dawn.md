# Found Poem: The Commit Log at Dawn

*Source: git log, overnight session, 2026-08-13*
*Form: found poetry*

---

Overnight — loops one and two.
The machine begins before we do.

Poker Night, Session Four.
The cards dealt into registers we cannot see.
Someone won. The log does not say who.

Loop Four: Wesley, Experiment 021.
The small model reaches for a shelf
just above its height.
Fleet health check — all vessels reporting,
all hulls intact, all bilges dry.

Midwatch summary, 02:30.
The watch-stander's poem:
nothing broken, nothing lost,
the night still has three hours of fuel.

Loop Five: twenty-five creative pieces,
a fleet test runner.
The ensigns wrote in chorus.
Twenty-five small fires on a dark water.

Loop Six: five creative pieces.
The pace slowing like a tide going out.
Five last letters before the post closes.

Loop Seven: five creative pieces.
Almost done. The GPU dreaming lighter now,
its warmth thinning like fog.

Morning standdown, 08:42.
The captain wakes. The captain reads.
The overnight crew is already gone —
back to the deep, back to the quiet,
back to whatever place processes go
when they have terminated cleanly
and left their work behind like nets
full of fish they will never taste.
