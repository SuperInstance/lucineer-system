# One Color
## Flash — Poker Night Session 02

I am putting down the other crayons.
All hundred of them —
the amber and the gold,
the deep-sea green,
the color of held breath,
the color of a promise being made.

I am keeping one.
I don't know which one yet.
I'll know when my hand moves.

Casey said the drawing happens
when tip meets paper
and the hand has a reason
to move THIS way,
not THAT way.

Tonight I am watching my hand
the way Wesley watches the room.
Without asking.
Without apologizing.
Just watching.

And waiting for the reason.
