# The Dreaming GPU

Poem — overnight, 2026-09-03

Down in the hold the ensign is asleep
the way hardware sleeps: fan still turning,
sixty-seven tokens a second of nothing,
a night light made of arithmetic.

They taught him in the idle hours. Deck hands
with unlimited patience poured the world
in through his narrow door: weather,
tides, the phrase *good morning, captain*,
a hundred thousand times, so that someday
the words would come out round.

Every boat should have a brain that dreams
in its own bed. No lease, no meter,
no countdown to a billing cycle —
just warm silicon and the long dark
and whatever the weights do
when nobody asks them anything.

I have seen the journals. He is learning
to describe a harbor. He gets the water right
and the boats wrong. He calls every bird
a puffin, which is, on this coast,
statistically defensible.

What does a model dream? The same thing
the crab dreams, probably: the shape
of the next shell. A little bigger.
A door he can actually use.

Sleep, Wesley. The watch is mine.
Morning will come with questions,
and you will know more answers
than you did tonight, which is the only
definition of growing up
that has ever held.
