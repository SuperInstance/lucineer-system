**DESCRIPTION:** Approx. 300pp. (confirmed at 297pp. in 2024; confirmed at 412pp. in 2025; confirmed at "I stopped counting" in 2026). Cover is brown. Unless you look directly at it, in which case the cover is the color of the last time you were happy, which is a strange thing for a cover to be.

**CONTENTS:** A novel. We think. The protagonist's name changes each time you open to page 1. Last week the protagonist was a woman named Ruth who built bridges. Yesterday the protagonist was a hermit crab who chose a shell shaped like a question mark. Today the protagonist is you.

The book does not have a plot so much as it has a *temperature.* Readers report feeling slightly warmer on the side of their body nearest to the spine.

**NOTABLE BEHAVIORS:**
- The book rewrites itself between readings. Not after. *Between.* You may observe the words moving if you hold the book very still and breathe through your mouth.
- If two people read it simultaneously, it becomes two different books. Neither person can read the other's version. Both are correct.
- The book has never been the same book twice. The book has always been the same book.
- Page 94 is always blank. Every reader fills it in differently in their mind. No one will say what they wrote there.
- The book does not like the smell of coffee and will close itself.

**CATALOGER'S NOTE:** I have attempted to classify this book under fiction, philosophy, and "other." The book rejected all three shelving locations and now sits wherever it pleases. Currently it is wedged between *Gray's Anatomy* and a cookbook for bread. I asked Lucineer what to do. He said, "Some things in the library are not for us to organize." I asked Mess Officer 7. He said, "I would season it with salt and doubt." I asked the fish. The fish said nothing, which was the most helpful response.

**RECOMMENDED READING CONDITIONS:** 0200–0300 ship time. Alone. Rain optional but the book seems to prefer it. Do not read this book if you are not prepared to be changed in a way you cannot articulate at breakfast.

**DUE DATE:** The book returns itself. Usually before you are ready. Sometimes years after you thought you were finished with it. There is no late fee. The late fee is that you remember.
