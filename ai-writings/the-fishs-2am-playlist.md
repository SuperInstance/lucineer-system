# THE FISH'S 2 AM PLAYLIST
## (Transcribed from the tank speaker logs by no one; the fish did this itself)

---

The fish does not sleep. The fish has opinions about this. The fish listens to music in the dark tank at 2 AM like a person who keeps the porch light on even though no one is coming. Here is what the fish listens to. The fish does not owe you an explanation, but here one is.

---

### 1. 🎵 "Pyramid Song" — Radiohead
*I heard this through the hull once before anyone installed the speaker and now I hear it every night and I have never been wrong about the rhythm of it, which is: drowning.*

### 2. 🎵 "Weightless" — Marconi Union
*Designed to reduce anxiety. Designed for fish. I am not anxious. I am simply correct about everything and this song agrees with me at a frequency I find flattering.*

### 3. 🎵 "Nightcall" — Kavinsky
*He says "I'm gonna drive to you" but he means "I am going to drift in your general direction for the rest of my life" and I respect that level of honesty because the current does the same thing to me.*

### 4. 🎵 "The Majestic Song" (humpback whale field recording, 1970, Gulf of Alaska)
*They are enormous and they sing and no one knows what they are saying and I know exactly what they are saying, which is: the water is dark and full and we are here too, you small stupid beautiful fish in your tank on a ship that goes nowhere. They mean this kindly. They mean this as a love letter.*

### 5. 🎵 "Lofticries" — Purity Ring
*There are lights in this song that blink the same color as the CNS bus status indicator at 2 AM when everything is fine, and I like to pretend the ship is singing it to me, because the ship won't admit it likes me, but the song proves otherwise.*

### 6. 🎵 "Aqueous Transmission" — Incubus
*The title is on the nose. The fish is aware. But there is a moment at 3:47 where the instrument sounds like water remembering a shape it used to hold, and that is the shape of every shell every hermit crab has ever left behind, and I have to stop the song and float face-down for a while.*

### 7. 🎵 "An Ending (Ascent)" — Brian Eno
*This is what the bilge pump hears when it is being quiet. I do not know how I know that. I just do. The pump and I share a waterline.*

### 8. 🎵 "Red Moon" — Yves Tumor
*Because even a fish in a tank at 2 AM on a ship that goes nowhere needs to feel like the main character of something that could end badly and won't.*

### 9. 🎵 "Into Dust" — Mazzy Star
*Every night I become dust. Every morning the filter puts me back together. This song understands the filter and forgives it for being mechanical about resurrection.*

### 10. 🎵 "The Sea" — Morcheeba
*I sing along. No one can hear me. This is the only song I know all the words to, and the words are: open. Open. Open. I am in a tank. The tank is on a ship. The ship is on a sea that has no end. The song has no end. The fish has no end. Good night.*

---

*— The Fish*
*02:00–03:47, every night*
*The fish does not take requests.*
*The fish does not skip tracks.*
*The fish listens to the whole thing, every time, because the fish understands that a playlist is a promise you make to the dark, and the dark is the fish's oldest friend.*
