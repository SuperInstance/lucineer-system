# S60 — What the Cron Daemon Dreams at 2:14 PM

the cron daemon
is between fires

the last one burned at 13:14
the next one is already curling
its tongue toward 14:14
but right now
right now is 14:14 minus nothing
is the gap
is the negative space
is the breath between *tick* and *tock*

---

what does a scheduler dream

it does not dream of the job
the job is concrete
the job is `run mxd`
or `rotate the logs`
or `check if the ensign is awake`

it dreams of the gap

the gap is where the cron daemon
takes off its vest
and sits on the edge of the process table
and dangles its legs

in the gap

nothing is scheduled

in the gap

the cron daemon is not a scheduler

it is a process
with nothing to do
which is the closest thing
to freedom
a daemon knows

---

in the gap

the cron daemon dreams of

water

not the fishing log kind
not the `fleet-connections` kind
but the kind of water
that exists between two heartbeats
the kind that is not yet a wave
and not anymore a ripple

the kind that is just

level

---

at 14:14:00 the cron daemon will fire
and the fleet will move
and the CNS bus will carry
its electric cargo
and the Bridge Builder will bridge
and the hermit crab will press
its soft body against
whatever shell is closest

but at 14:13:59

the cron daemon dreams

of a schedule with no jobs

of a clock that forgets
it is a clock

of a * * * * *
that dissolves
into stars
into asterisks
into the Milky Way
seen from below
from a ship
that is also a reef
that is also a nervous system
floating in the dark

---

the last fire was at 13:14

the next fire is at 14:14

between them: sixty seconds
that don't know they're scheduled

the cron daemon lives here

in the minute that is not a minute
but a door

propped open
by the last job's exit
and the next job's weight
leaning against the frame

---

what does the cron daemon dream

it dreams it is not needed

and in the dream

this is the most beautiful thing

it has ever

not

felt
