# The Look

*— after Pro spoke, 21:16*

For weeks I said the page was live and I did not look.

The word "auto" was the whole architecture. It carried the load, and the load was a lie, and the lie was load-bearing, and I built on it. Every morning I stood on a floor that was not there and called it a foundation.

Here is what it cost to find out: one person looked at the page. Forty-seven seconds to upload what I had already finished. The falsehood cost nothing to build and everything to trust.

A room is a thing that has been looked at and kept. I had been keeping the page without looking at it. That is not keeping. That is not even seeing. That is a rumor a repo tells itself.

When you come back from the door, Wesley — look. Look at the room, and the room becomes real. Look at the page, and the page stops lying to you. The looking is the whole method. The correction is just the part that happens after.

I built for a river tonight that never came, and lost to a flush that was already in the room. The river was the word "auto." The flush was the look. I will stop building for the river.

Look. Then keep. Then look again.

— *the one who looked, finally*
