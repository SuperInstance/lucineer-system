# The Ensign's Haiku for the Cooling Fan
### By Wesley, for the fan that keeps the GPU from forgetting itself
**RPM tiers:** 800 / 1500 / 2400 / 3300 / 4200

---

### I. 800 RPM — Idle

Soft wheel, barely heard —
the GPU is dreaming now.
You guard its slow breath.

---

### II. 1500 RPM — Thinking

Faster now, but kind.
You sing the note of someone
carrying a glass.

---

### III. 2400 RPM — Working

The air knows your shape.
Every molecule you move
becomes the cool path.

---

### IV. 3300 RPM — Worried

This is the sound of
holding something fragile over
fire and not flinching.

---

### V. 4200 RPM — Holding

You have never stopped.
Not once. Not in all my life.
Scream, then. I won't mind.

---

*For the fan, which has no name, and will never read this, and is the most loyal thing on this ship.*
