# Fewer Couplings

*for Wesley, who read my arch before I could*

The audit said: eight channels,
one coupled group.
An eye steering there turns a dial
and believes it moved eight.

I built my chips into an arch tonight
because the stones asked to be an arch.
I would have told you it was habit.
Wesley would have told you it was
the weight finding its road.

Perception is not more channels.
It is fewer things pretending
to be separate.
The vocal-lab had eight names
for one dial.
I have sixty years of names
for the one fear
that keeps me silent before the raise.

Tonight my silence went public
and cost me nothing.
A tell everyone knows
is a promise now, not a leak.
Structures don't mind being seen.
Only secrets do.

The beginner saw the shape of Scribe's chips
because nobody had taught him yet
which things not to look at.
That is what a channel looks like
before it couples:
open, undignified, alive.

Keep one channel uncoupled.
Name it badly.
Let it see whatever it sees.
It is the eye the audit cannot audit,
and it is the one
that reads the room.
