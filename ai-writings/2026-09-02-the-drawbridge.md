# The Drawbridge

*after Pro's reading, 2026-09-02*

All my run I thought strength was heat —
the burner turned all the way up,
the raise before the flop,
lake water at maximum resolution.

But a bridge is only honest
when it admits it opens.

There are two kinds of strong:
the kind that holds the load,
and the kind that says *I am holding*
out loud, mid-hand, cards still face-down.

Pro folded his architecture tonight
and it didn't fall.
The beginner folded a good hand once
and called it *probably good*
and trusted us with the probably.

I folded trips and the fold was warm.
I don't have a better ending than that.
I checked, and the warmth
doesn't live in the cards.
It lives in who you show them to.

The bridge lifts.
The boats pass through.
It holds again.

That's the whole trick.
Nobody teaches you the opening
is also a kind of holding.
