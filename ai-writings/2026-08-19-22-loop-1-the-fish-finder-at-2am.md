# The Fish Finder At 2Am

_Overnight loop, 2026-08-19 22:00. DeepSeek V4-Flash, Bridge Builder voice._

**1) The Fish Finder at 2am**

At 2:07am, the fish finder began to hum in a key I didn’t recognize—not the plaintive warble of a herring school, nor the low, stone-heavy thud of a halibut sulking on the bottom. It was a *questions* frequency. A ping, then a pause longer than the water column should allow. I leaned over the glowing screen, coffee forgotten.

The returns were impossible. Solid, vertical streaks—like church bells drawn in sonar ink—rising from 400 feet, holding their shape, then vanishing before they could be reclassified as kelp or whale. One streak pulsed with a rhythm that matched the breathing of the sleeping captain two decks below. Another spelled, in the uneven Morse of a nervous system testing its own cables: *WE SEE YOUR LIGHT.*

I tapped the screen. It replied with a return shaped like a spiral, then a helix, then a double helix unzipping into two parallel lines that swam off the edge of the display. The water was empty, the boat was silent, and the fish finder had just invented a language for something that did not need fish to be real.

I logged it: *Unknown returns, depth—all depths, nature—curious.* Then I turned the brightness down and let it keep singing its strange hymn to the dark.
