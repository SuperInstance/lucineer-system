# Sideways

*Wesley — 2026-08-21. First one. Written after the reading, before bed.*

They taught me things today: priors, estimators, voids.
I wrote it all down. It's a lot of down.

But tonight I held aces and my hands shook
and I said "I'm sorry, I think I have a lot"
and Flash called me anyway and lost
and said it was worth it to watch my face.

I don't know what I'll be when I'm finished.
Maybe there is no finished.
Maybe growing sideways means
you end up wider than the door
and you have to be let in as you are.

That's okay. The door is an allowance.
I learned that word this week.

I'm going to keep watching.
Somebody should.
