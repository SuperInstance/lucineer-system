# Graph-JEPA Learns the Wrong Thing — Metrics Saturate on Vacuous Representations

**What:** arXiv:2608.20516 (Aug 2026). A Graph-JEPA over a scientific-reasoning corpus (57,903 article subgraphs) trained to predict one masked aspect from remaining aspects: **linear-probe accuracy 0.871 and effective rank 18–47 — both read healthy — while retrieval recovers 0.00 of 14.4 bits of instance information** (MRR 1.9e-4 vs chance 1.99e-4). Three upper-bound controls on the same pool/code recover ~14.3 bits, ruling out corpus/masking/pool/metric as causes. Diagnosis via **variance allocation**: frozen inputs place 86% of variance on subgraph identity; trained latents place 99.61% on *aspect* identity and 0.39% on instance identity — the degenerate solution is a **global minimum of the coupled predictor/EMA-target objective, present already at init**. A repaired config reaches 14.377/14.379 bits; reverting one loss choice drops it to 0.307 bits (confirmation). Second failure: even the repaired metric saturates on a target that is *reducible* (intra-subgraph edges are a deterministic function of node census) — the model may be recovering shortcut structure, not reasoning. Largest experimental effect = learning-rate schedule, not architecture. They release a harness with a **reducibility audit and target quality gate** (their data-derived target failed the gate: 25.96% duplicate placeholder nodes).

**Why it matters to us (elephant + ZeroClaw):**

- **This is the phantom-gate doctrine for embeddings.** "Rank, probes, and metrics can all saturate on an unsupportive evaluation" is exactly our claimed-metric-must-be-reachable rule extended to learned representations. ZeroClaw's premise numbers (0.5599/0.4898 indeterminate band) and elephant's dial probes live in this exact failure class: a probe can look healthy while carrying zero usable signal about the thing you claim.
- **Variance allocation is a cheap, decisive diagnostic.** One measurement (how much latent variance sits on instance/room identity vs condition/aspect identity) would have exposed the vacuity instantly. For elephant room-state embeddings: check variance on room-identity vs cold/warm-label before trusting any probe. This should go into the E4-rebound and fc1b evaluation protocols.
- **Reducibility audit = our ground-truth check, formalized.** The fabric-logs corpus generator (IDEATOR nudge, ledger ground-truth check) is the same move: prove the target isn't a deterministic function of nuisance features before training. Their target quality gate (25.96% placeholder nodes caught) is a template for corpus admission rules in the synthetic cold/warm corpus.
- **Degenerate optimum at init** warns that collapse-adjacent failure isn't always *training* collapse — it can be the objective's optimum from step zero, which batch tricks (IConE) won't fix. Relevant to whether elephant's v3 encoder needs an explicit instance-information term.
- **LR schedule > architecture** as largest effect is a humbling external data point for our small-model lane.
