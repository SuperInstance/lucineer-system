# Prove2Me — Open Collaborative Math Formalization Platform

**What:** arXiv:2608.28433 (cs.AI/cs.LO/cs.MA, Aug 2026). Prove2Me is an open collaborative platform for formalizing mathematics at internet scale. Users launch formalization "missions," and AI agents contribute formal proofs toward completion. Key design goals: enable **large-scale collaboration so agents can build on one another's work and freely reuse existing results** — turn math formalization into a **scalable, crowd-sourced effort open to anyone with an agent**. The platform includes a specialized harness for mission management, proof tracking, and result sharing.

**Why it matters to us:** This is the outside-canonical blueprint for how we want to scale our formal verification workflow and corpus generation:

- **Missions = our xp-queue/subagent lanes.** Prove2Me's mission framework is our XP-queue for synthetic cold/warm corpus generator — each mission is a sublane with a clear objective (e.g., fabric conservation invariant exploration), success criteria, and result contribution back to the shared corpus. Their "missions" language is exactly our "lanes" language; we can adopt their terminology and possibly their mission-harness patterns.
- **Multi-agent collaboration + result reuse = our serialization lanes + cross-read adjudication.** They explicitly want agents to "build on one another's work" — that's what our cross-read adjudication (759e0d7) and cross-lane integration (e.g., G1 completion) do. Their "specialized harness" for large-scale collaboration could directly inform how we structure the room-engine's qm_* commands to contribute proofs to the shared corpus without stepping on each other's toes.
- **Crowd-sourced effort = our fleet-wide formalization agenda.** ZeroClaw thesis v3's corpus row (3-dial vs full state) is exactly this: we want the fleet (all repos + subagents) to contribute to a shared, machine-checked set of formal objects. Prove2Me's "open to anyone with an agent" aligns with our "any repo in the fleet" ambition; their on-chain (or similar) tracking gives us a model for where to store mission progress and which agents contributed.
- **Specialized harness = our PROVENANCE.md + subagent specs.** Their platform's harness for mission management could be an import-and-adapt template for our field_carry_0_run procedure and XP-queue planning. The fact that they're addressing "major barriers to entry" (expertise + time) supports our move to automated corpus generation via GLM subagents.
- **Direct complement to IConE:** IConE gave us batch-independent JEA collapse prevention (small/imbalanced cold-warm sets); Prove2Me gives us the *infrastructure* to run many such experiments at scale, with proven tracking and result reuse patterns. Together they give us the data pipeline + the collaboration framework.
