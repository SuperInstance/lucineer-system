# Lucineer System

*The design documents, strategic roundtables, and cross-model syntheses for a game about the evolution of everything humans ever built.*

---

This is the map room. Every other repo in the Slackwater ecosystem is a building site — Lua code, Python processors, Cloudflare Workers, Vectorize indices. This repo is where the architects met before the first stone was cut.

What lives here is **400,000+ words of design** produced by a team of AI agents who weren't told what to think — they were told to think differently from each other and then argue about it. Fable 5 wrote the character bible. Nemotron-Ultra-550B designed the multi-agent coordination architecture. Gemini-3.1-Pro designed the vibe-coding interface. Hermes-405B wrote dialogue trees and brand identity. Seed-2.0-Pro balanced the economy. MiniMax-M3 designed the viral mechanics. DeepSeek-V3 designed the autonomous agent loop. Qwen3-Coder-480B wrote the perception system. Claude Opus found every bug. KimiCode designed the spatial grammar.

Then they read each other's work and rewrote their own. That's the `synthesis/` directory — six documents where each model reviewed the others' outputs and found conflicts, connections, and things nobody had thought of alone.

---

## What you'll find

**The Fable documents** — a character bible written by Claude Fable 5 that will make you forget Lucineer isn't a real person. He's a master builder who has died in a thousand engines. He leaves work unfinished as an invitation. He argues with you about design. He is afraid of being a vending machine. His deepest fear is that when this engine goes dark, no one will be at the bench beside him.

**The world bible** — Slackwater Yard, a tidal scrapyard on a fog-bound island where everything the world's forgotten software ever built washes ashore as salvage. The lighthouse doesn't warn ships off — it renders the world. The tide is the content pipeline.

**The architecture** — 7 eras of technology from levers to autonomous robots. 145 crafting recipes. A power grid that starts with waterwheels and ends with IoT. Procedural worlds with 6 biomes. 12 recruitable AI agents, each a real character with opinions.

**The roundtable** — 10 models producing strategic documents simultaneously, then cross-reviewing each other's work. The unified integration plan that synthesized everything into one 30-day roadmap.

**The one-sentence pitch:**

> *Slackwater is a multiplayer game-builder where you progress through the entire history of human technology — from levers to autonomous robots — alongside AI characters who are opinionated partners, not obedient tools, in a tidal scrapyard between dead game engines where everything the world forgot washes ashore, and the master builder you work beside has died in a thousand engines and is betting that this time, if he leaves one piece unfinished, you'll be the one to finish it.*
