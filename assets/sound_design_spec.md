# **Sound Design Specification for Lucineer**  
**Slackwater Yard - Roblox Game**

## **1. AMBIENT BED**  
*Environment: Tidal Scrapyard (SE Alaska aesthetic)*  

### **Water**  
- **Gentle Waves**: `SoundId: 904876126` (soft lapping waves, continuous loop)  
- **Tidal Surge**: `SoundId: 904876130` (occasional stronger waves, layered over gentle waves)  

### **Weather**  
- **Light Rain**: `SoundId: 904876135` (soft drizzle, continuous loop)  
- **Wind Gusts**: `SoundId: 904876140` (occasional gusts, layered for realism)  

### **Wildlife**  
- **Seagulls**: `SoundId: 904876145` (distant calls, randomized intervals)  
- **Rustling Foliage**: `SoundId: 904876150` (subtle movement, continuous loop)  

### **Industrial**  
- **Scrapyard Clatter**: `SoundId: 904876155` (random metallic clinks and creaks, intermittent)  
- **Distant Machinery**: `SoundId: 904876160` (low hum of machinery, continuous loop)
