# Slackwater Yard — Concept Art Prompts

---

### 1. The Cannery Forge at Dusk
Wide establishing shot looking up the cannery ramp as the forge glows amber against a deep teal twilight sky. Embers drift lazily from the open bay doors, casting long warm light across wet plank decking and coiled ropes. Palette: burnt orange, oxidized iron, muted indigo. Atmosphere: end-of-shift stillness, the last working fire in a fading world.

### 2. The Dock with Capitaine Tender
Low-angle three-quarter view from the waterline, the weathered tender *Capitaine* tied slack against creosote pilings, her hull reflecting bruised violet and rust. Cold morning fog thickens the background while pilot-house windows offer a single yellow square of warmth. Palette: steel gray, barnacle green, candleflame yellow. Mood: patient, salt-bitten, waiting for no one in particular.

### 3. Lighthouse Beam in Fog
Dramatic perspective from inside the lantern room looking outward through iron-framed glass, the beam slicing volumetric fog into geometric shafts of pale gold. Salt-streaked lens and copper fittings catch the rotating light in rhythmic flashes. Palette: charcoal, bone-white, tarnished brass. Mood: solitary vigilance, the slow heartbeat of an obsolete machine.

### 4. Lucineer at His Anvil
Medium close-up of the Lucineer mid-strike, sparks spraying in a clean arc across a soot-blackened shop interior lit only by forge glow and a high clerestory window. His silhouette is industrial-blocky, forearms thick with leather bracers, an expression of focused calm. Palette: ember red, gunmetal, hot-white sparks. Angle: slightly below, to make the labor feel monumental.

### 5. Tideline After Ebb
Eye-level shoreline view of the exposed intertidal zone, kelp ribbons, broken net floats, and a half-buried brass fitting glistening on dark sand. Distant yard structures sit low and small against a flat overcast sky, the tide pulled out like a held breath. Palette: olive-drab, wet slate, fish-silver. Mood: patient recession, the world showing its working bones.

### 6. Player + Lucineer Carrying a Beam
Two-figure composition shot from behind and slightly below, the player avatar and Lucineer hauling a salvaged timber beam across a catwalk, the beam cutting a strong diagonal across the frame. Backlit by a low sun, their forms are heavy and determined, shadows stretching long across planking. Palette: sepia, copper, dust-gold. Mood: small figures, large work, earned partnership.

### 7. Earl at His Manifest
Over-the-shoulder interior shot of Earl hunched at a clipboard-thick ledger inside a cramped, paper-strewn office, a single hanging bulb throwing a cone of warm yellow against corrugated walls. Ink bottles, a tin mug, and a mounted fish trophy crowd the desk edges. Palette: tobacco brown, parchment cream, lampfilament gold. Mood: bureaucratic warmth, the quiet keel of a working enterprise.

### 8. Aurora Over the Yard
High wide shot from the ridge above Slackwater, the yard spread below as a small constellation of warm windows against black water, while a green-violet aurora ribbons overhead. The contrast between the handmade amber glow below and the cosmic display above sets the tone of small human industry against vast wilderness. Palette: aurora green, magnesium violet, forge amber, ink black. Mood: awe tempered by familiarity.

### 9. Completed Castle on the Ridge
High-angle revealshot from a treeline overlook, the player's finished driftwood castle perched on the grassy ridge, sailcloth flags limp in dead air, a small lantern glowing in its central keep. The yard lies small and safe below, docklines and pilings reduced to tidy geometry. Palette: weathered gray, moss green, lantern gold, faded red. Mood: quiet triumph, a structure that looks like it grew there.

### 10. The Storm
Dynamic low-angle shot from the leeward side of the cannery, horizontal rain sheeting across the yard, pilings straining, the *Capitaine* bucking against her lines. Lightning freezes the scene in a single hard frame: white sky, black water, the silhouette of the Lucineer bracing a tarp over the forge. Palette: ink, lightning white, sodium orange, gunmetal. Mood: the yard tested, stubbornly still standing.
