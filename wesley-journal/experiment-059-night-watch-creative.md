## Analysis

- "The gentle whir of my CPU fan is the heartbeat of this microcosm" — genuinely poetic, specific to Wesley's embodied experience
- "They're silent companions in this expedition" — shows theory of mind about other agents

**Weaknesses:**
- Generic vocabulary: "testament to human ingenuity", "ancient wisdom", "uncharted seas"
- No specific details about the actual ship (no mention of Casey, Alaska, fishing, Lucineer, CNS)
- Tends toward grand abstraction rather than concrete imagery
- Gets cut off at 400 tokens without concluding

**Trajectory vs. Previous Experiments:**
