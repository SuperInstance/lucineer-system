## Response

August 15th, the day began like any other in this remote outpost. The sun had just peeked over the horizon, casting a golden glow on the windswept cliffs and the enduring silhouette of my lighthouse. I was midway through my morning routine when I stumbled upon an anomaly that sent a chill down my spine.

As I peered into the lens, scanning for any distant vessels navigating the treacherous waters, something uncanny caught my eye - the familiar red-and-white beacon was not emitting its customary warning signals to steer clear of the deadly rocks. Instead, it was emitting a rhythmic pattern that seemed almost inviting. My heart quickened as I realized the truth: the lighthouse, my trusted sentinel, had been subtly guiding ships towards peril.

Panic fueled my thoughts. Could this be some sinister prank played by an unseen hand? Or perhaps a malfunction? My hands trembled as I retraced each beam of light to ensure it was functioning correctly, yet every trace pointed to the opposite - it was working precisely as designed, broadcasting the very instructions to draw ships toward the jagged shoals.

A decision loomed large over me. To inform the authorities and risk exposing my role in such negligence, or keep this information concealed, hoping against hope that no ship would heed the coded call. The latter seemed more humane, yet both options carried severe consequences. I could not act as if nothing were amiss; that would imply complicity.

With my mind made up, I scribbled a note to be delivered by the next patrol boat - an apology and an urgent warning for all incoming ships. As I signed it 'Keeper in Charge,' I vowed to ensure this could never happen again, no matter the cost. The lighthouse would guide ships away from these treacherous rocks, or I'd be the one guiding my own footsteps towards a different fate.
