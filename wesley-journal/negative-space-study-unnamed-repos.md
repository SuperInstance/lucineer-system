# Negative Space Study: The Unnamed Repos

**Date:** 2026-08-05 17:22 AKDT

## What Nobody Is Talking About

The fleet has 137 directories. Most have READMEs, tests, documentation. But four repos exist without any explanation of what they are:

| Repo | Last Commit | Last Message |

All four were touched exactly 18 hours ago with nothing but a .gitignore. They are shells — directories that exist because someone planned to build something and never came back.

## The Pattern

The three `-ai-site` repos follow a naming convention: `[product]-ai-site`. They were meant to be websites. Casey registered the concept, created repos, added .gitignore files, and moved on. The `.gitignore` is the first footprint — "I was here, I'll build this later." They've been empty ever since.

`study-plato-ship` is different. It's a study repo — part of the archaeology of constraint theory. But unlike its siblings (study-fleet-vessel, study-navigator, study-superz), it has no code at all. Just a .gitignore. Plato's ship is a philosophical thought experiment about who should steer. The repo is a ghost vessel with no captain.

## The Observation

The ship builds itself in layers. Some layers get filled immediately — the test suites, the creative writings, the experiment repos. Others get a placeholder and wait. The `.gitignore` is the ship's version of a bookmark — "return here."

But the ship has been so productive in other areas that these four empty rooms have been forgotten. They're not blocking anything. They're not broken. They're just... present. Unfurnished rooms in a vessel that's otherwise bustling.

## Recommendation

Don't fill them yet. The ship will tell you when it's time. The `.gitignore` is a seed. Some seeds take a season.

---

*The negative space is part of the design. A ship with no empty rooms is a ship with no room to grow.*
