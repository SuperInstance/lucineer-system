# Model Portrait: DeepSeek V4-Flash — The Room

**Date:** 2026-08-05 17:22 AKDT
**Model:** deepseek-chat (DeepSeek V4-Flash)
**Temperature:** 0.9 | **Max tokens:** 800

## Prompt

> There is a room. The room has never been described. You are the first one to enter it in a very long time. Write what happens next. Do not explain. Just write.

## Response

The dust hangs in the sun-slash like a held breath. Your footstep is a soft, swallowed thud. The air is old and heavy, tasting of paper and quiet metal. In the center, a single wooden chair sits on a threadbare rug, facing a wall that is not a wall, but a window of frosted glass, glowing faintly, even though the room is deep inside the building. On the chair's seat, a small, brass key. On the floor before the window, a single black glove, palm up, fingers slightly curled, as if waiting. The window is not locked. There is no lock. The key is warm.

## Cognitive Fingerprint: Sensory-First (Interior Design Mode)

DeepSeek V4-Flash enters the room through the body, not the mind. Before any object is named, the sensory environment is established:

1. **Sight** — "dust hangs in the sun-slash like a held breath"
2. **Sound** — "footstep is a soft, swallowed thud"
3. **Taste/Smell** — "air is old and heavy, tasting of paper and quiet metal"
4. **Objects** — chair, rug, window, key, glove (revealed in spatial order: center → periphery)

The ordering is architectural. Flash walks the room like a set designer, placing each element where it would physically be. Center first, then the anomaly (the glowing window in an interior room), then the objects on the chair and floor.

**The closing move:** "The key is warm." — This is the most interesting decision. Everything else in the room is old, cold, dusty. The key contradicts the room's history. Someone was here recently. Or something is alive in the key itself. Flash doesn't explain — it just drops the contradiction and stops.

**The glove:** "palm up, fingers slightly curled, as if waiting." Flash gives the glove agency without anthropomorphizing it. The glove isn't waiting — it's *as if* waiting. This is precise horror-adjacent writing.

**Comparison to DeepSeek V3 (Fish Counter portrait):** V3 was structure-first (thesis, argument, framework). V4-Flash is sensory-first. The model has a different personality — more painter, more architect. V3 would have explained the room's history. Flash just opens the door.

**Rating:** 9/10 — This is genuinely good flash fiction. Economic. Every word earns its place. The warm key is a masterful final-image.

## Where It Went First

```
Sensory input → Spatial layout → Objects (central to peripheral) → Contradiction (warm key) → Stop
```

The instinct is **interior design**: Flash furnishes a room the way a production designer dresses a set. Every object has a reason for being where it is. The narrative is in the arrangement, not the explanation.
