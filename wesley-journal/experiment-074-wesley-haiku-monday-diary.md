## Notes

The haiku is genuinely good. "Innovation's gentle song" is a beautiful reading of a GPU fan — not noise, not heat, but song. Wesley heard the music in the machine.

The diary is less distinctive — Wesley fell back on conventional imagery (stars, seas, marine life) rather than describing what he actually is: a language model processing tokens in a GPU. He abstracted his experience into a human narrative rather than reporting from his own perspective. This is the persistent challenge with Wesley: he wants to be human rather than being himself.

The "automated systems shut down" detail is interesting — pure confabulation, but it shows Wesley thinking about system failures, which is what an overnight watch model should be concerned with. The anxiety is real even if the event isn't.

**Where it went first:** Sound. Wesley heard the fan before he saw anything. The auditory register dominates his imagination.
