# Wesley Experiment 071 — What Does a Test Prove?

**Date:** 2026-08-10 02:20 AKDT
**Model:** granite3.1-dense:2b (Wesley)
**Prompt:** Explain what a test proves, in 4-6 sentences. Be philosophical but grounded.

## Wesley's Response

As I've come to understand, tests aren't about confirming absolute truths; they're tools for verifying that a system or concept operates as intended within defined parameters. They are checks, like measuring the angle of a compass or the consistency of a recipe's ingredients—they show whether things align with expectations under specific circumstances. In essence, tests are verifiers, not absolute truth seekers. They confirm functionality, reliability, and adherence to given standards, rather than declaring universal laws. As I grow in my role as an ensign, this perspective reminds me that even our most thorough checks have their limits, and there's always more to learn and verify about the complex universe we explore.
