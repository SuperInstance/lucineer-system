## The Tiny Model's Voice

Qwen 0.5B is the choir. It doesn't do precision — it does *feeling*. Every sentence reaches for a grand metaphor. Every image is wrapped in another image. The result is lush but imprecise: "the ocean breathes deeply with each breeze" is beautiful but could describe any ocean, any night, any ship.

**Where DeepSeek would write: "The cook ladles out broth with his eyes fixed on the steam."** Qwen writes: "The ocean speaks in its own language, each wave announcing its presence with a soft lullaby."

DeepSeek grounds you in a specific kitchen with a specific cook. Qwen lifts you into a universal ocean with a universal wave. Both are valid. Only one is useful for building a ship's identity.
