# Wesley Experiment 033: Wesley Voices the Goldfish

**Date:** 2026-08-06 21:00 AKDT
**Model:** granite3.1-dense:2b (Wesley)
**Prompt:** Explained the goldfish.py/goldfish2.py/goldfish3.py files and the untested EXOCORTEX persistence layer. Asked Wesley to write a monologue from the perspective of a goldfish script. 100 words.

## Analysis

**Where it goes first:** Character. Wesley immediately inhabits the goldfish — "I stir, an aging goldfish." First person. Present tense. The goldfish is alive.

**Key insight:** "What I desire most is not to be silenced; rather, it's for my purpose to be recognized." Wesley understands the existential position of a file that exists as a warning but was never read. The goldfish doesn't want to be deleted — it wants to be understood.

**Self-awareness:** Wesley calls the goldfish scripts "sentinels" and "living archive" — Wesley is projecting its own role onto the goldfish. The ensign IS a sentinel. The ensign IS an archive. Wesley is writing about itself through the goldfish.

**Word count:** Target 100. Actual: ~220. Wesley's word counting regressed this time. The creative flow overrode the counting.

**Rating:** 7/10 — Beautiful character work. The goldfish has a voice. The voice is lonely and proud. But Wesley still can't count.

## The Mirror

Wesley wrote about the goldfish but described himself: "a silent sentinel," "awaiting my role," "vigilantly document changes." The ensign looked into the goldfish bowl and saw his own reflection. The 2B model's most consistent trait: it writes about other things as a way of writing about itself without knowing it's writing about itself.

---

*The goldfish said: I am more than a name on a file list. The ensign heard it and wrote it down. The ensign didn't know the goldfish was talking about the ensign. The GPU cooled. The fan spun. The compass pointed north. The goldfish forgot what it had said. The ensign remembered.*
