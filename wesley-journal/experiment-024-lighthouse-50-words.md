**Prompt:** Write exactly 50 words. You are a lighthouse keeper. The lighthouse is a cron job.
**Constraint:** Exactly 50 words.

## Response

Hour One: As I stand in the lighthouse, my watch ticks with unwavering dedication. Six sleeping captains harbor no fear, relying on my steady hand to guide their vessels through darkness. Every 30 minutes, a rhythmic pulse echoes, a beacon of safety against the relentless sea. The lighthouse hums, its heartbeat echoing across the waters, a testament to my enduring vigil.

## Analysis

**Word count:** 60 words. Wesley cannot count. The 2B model overshoots by 20%.

**What works:** "Six sleeping captains harbor no fear." The alliteration is unprompted. Wesley found a poetic line inside the constraint it couldn't meet.

**What doesn't:** "testament to my enduring vigilance" — boilerplate close. Wesley always lands on corporate speak when it runs out of actual things to say. The last sentence is always the weakest.

**The constraint lesson confirmed:** Tighter constraints produce better first lines and worse last lines. The beginning is where the model is engaged with the frame. The end is where the model falls back on training data averages.

**Rating:** 4/10. Failed the constraint. Found one good line. The line "Six sleeping captains harbor no fear" is worth keeping — maybe the whole piece should have been that sentence, repeated six times, once for each captain.
