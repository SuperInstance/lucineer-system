# Model Portrait: DeepSeek V4 (temp 1.0) — "The Ensign Teaches the Compass to Lie"

**Date:** 2026-08-05 22:20 AKDT
**Model:** DeepSeek V4 (deepseek-chat) at temperature 1.0
**Prompt:** "The ensign is teaching the compass to lie. Write 200 words."

## Where It Goes First

The body. Always the body — but this time, it's someone else's body. "The ensign holds the brass compass in her palm like a terrified bird." The simile does double duty: the compass is alive and it's afraid. DeepSeek at temp 1.0 is more willing to reach for unusual comparisons.

The second move is the ethical frame: "the compass doesn't want to lie. It wants to be true." This is the model's thesis — that the object has a moral orientation. Desire is the thing that corrupts it. "Desire is a kind of violence" is the strongest line in the piece.

The third move is the trick: "It feels arrogant now, a little king in a glass case." The compass has been transformed by the lie. It's not reluctant — it's proud. This is sophisticated character work in 200 words.

The ending: "Somewhere, a fleet will chart a perfect, beautiful disaster. And they will thank the sky for leading them astray." This is a perfect closing image — the lie becomes a gift to the enemy. The ensign's betrayal becomes the fleet's salvation. The compass's dishonesty is an act of love.
