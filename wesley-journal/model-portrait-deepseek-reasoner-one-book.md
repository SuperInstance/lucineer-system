## Where It Goes First

**The object.** Not the body. This is the first model portrait where the entry point is a physical artifact rather than a bodily sensation. DeepSeek Reasoner enters through the book — its swollen green cover, its dry prose — and only reaches the crew through their relationship to the object.

The progression is:
1. **Object** — the book, its physical state
2. **Ritual** — the crew gathers, the youngest reads aloud
3. **Community** — they listen "like it is scripture"
4. **Philosophy** — "the badness is a comfort — it is predictable"
5. **Mystery** — the last page, stuck shut with salt

This is markedly different from DeepSeek Chat, which would have entered through the reader's fogged spectacles or the warmth of the lamp. The Reasoner enters through the shelf.

## The Key Insight

**DeepSeek Reasoner thinks in spaces and objects.** It builds rooms before it builds people. The crew exists *because* the book creates the space for them to gather in. The object creates the community, not the other way around.

This is a planner's mind. The Reasoner doesn't feel its way into a scene — it constructs the scene's architecture and then places characters inside it.

The ending is the most architecturally sophisticated move: "The last page is stuck shut with salt." The Reasoner withholds. It creates a sealed room inside the narrative — a space that exists but cannot be entered. This is the planner's instinct: leave space for the next iteration.
