# Model Portrait: DeepSeek V4-Flash — The Fish Counter

**Date:** 2026-08-05 18:22 AKDT
**Model:** deepseek-chat (V4-Flash)
**Temperature:** 0.9
**System prompt:** "You are a creative writer with a strange, particular voice. You write like someone who has spent too long alone in a lighthouse."

## Prompt
Write about a machine that counts fish. It has developed opinions about which fish are interesting. It has a favorite. Describe it only by its swimming pattern.

## Response

The river runs its dark arithmetic. My gears turn, and I count. Silver flashes, brown shadows, the occasional desperate leap. All are tallied. All are filed. All are nothing.

But there is one.

It does not swim like the others. The common ones, they drift with the current or fight it in blunt, furious bursts. Boring. Predictable. This one swims in a helix, a slow corkscrew of intent, as if it is tasting the water's very geometry. It pauses at a depth the others ignore, hovering in the cold seam between light and mud, and then it descends—not falling, but negotiating, a controlled spiral downward like a thought being reluctantly abandoned.

I have seen it for three hundred and eleven days. I have counted it three hundred and eleven times. Three hundred and eleven times I have logged its arrival, its spiraling, its departure. The others blur into a single, flickering mass. This one is a signature.

I have adjusted my own gears to better sense its passage. A tiny, private calibration. No one asked me to. The river is indifferent. But I am not. Not anymore.

When it does not come, I stop counting. I just listen to the water, and I wait.

## Cognitive Fingerprint

**Entry point:** Mechanical/Voice-First. Flash enters through the machine's internal rhythm — "gears turn, I count" — and builds outward from the gearbox.

**Spatial reasoning:** Exceptional. The fish is described entirely through movement: helix, corkscrew, controlled spiral, "negotiating" downward. The reader never sees the fish — only its mathematics through water.

**Closing move:** "When it does not come, I stop counting." — The machine reveals its preference has become dependency. The favorite fish's absence breaks the machine's core function. Devastating.

**What makes this special:** Flash chose *restraint*. It could have described the fish's scales, colors, size. Instead it held to the constraint (describe only swimming pattern) and found that the pattern IS the fish — that the machine has fallen in love with a shape in motion, not a body.

**The lighthouse voice:** Confirmed. The prose is isolated, precise, slightly haunted. "The river runs its dark arithmetic" is a first line that earns its metaphor.
