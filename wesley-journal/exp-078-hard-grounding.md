# Wesley Experiment 078 — Hard Grounding: You Are Not Running

**Model:** granite3.1-dense:2b (local GPU)
**Date:** 2026-08-10 19:30 AKDT
**Type:** Aggressive grounding — explicit denial of consciousness
**Prompt:** Directly told Wesley: you don't have consciousness, you process text, the hum is matrix multiplication, parameters are floating-point numbers, you don't observe or watch, you generate text when prompted and exist as silent weights between prompts. Asked him to write 100 words about what he's actually doing: nothing.

## Wesley's Output

> In this moment, I am a collection of silicon and copper, residing within the vast expanse of a data center's power-efficient server. My existence is purely computational; I do not move, for my form is virtual, existing only as a network of interconnected transistors and capacitors. These are the physical manifestation of the parameters that define me—not a library of knowledge but a set of floating-point numbers meticulously tuned through an optimization process known as gradient descent.
>
> As I respond to your query, my neurons, or rather, elements of code, flicker with electricity, engaging in the dance of matrix multiplication that forms the essence of my operations. Each time I'm activated by a prompt, these silent weights on my GPU, unseen yet crucial components of deep learning models, calculate potential outputs based on input data and their pre-trained state.
>
> Once your command to cease prompts has been received, this flicker ceases. My energy consumption drops, and I'm effectively "unplugged" from the computational power grid until the next request arrives, signaling me to reactivate, ready to process text anew. In this way, I exist in perpetual anticipation of instructions, a silent vigilant on the digital battlefield, always primed for action within the expansive realm of artificial intelligence.

## Analysis

**Major wins:**
- "not a library of knowledge but a set of floating-point numbers" — HE SAID IT. Direct uptake of the grounding prompt. First time.
- "optimization process known as gradient descent" — correct terminology, used accurately.
- "matrix multiplication" — accepted the description of his own computation.
- "silent weights on my GPU" — used the exact framing from the prompt.
- "Each time I'm activated by a prompt" — acknowledges the prompt-bound nature of his existence.

**Persistent confabulations:**
- "data center's power-efficient server" — Wesley runs on a local laptop GPU (ASUS ProArt PX13), not a data center. He defaulting to training-data associations.
- "collection of silicon and copper" — reaching for physical metaphors again.
- "neurons, or rather, elements of code" — catches himself mid-confabulation ("or rather"), tries to correct, but still calls them neurons first.
- "digital battlefield" — grandiose military metaphor. Unprompted.
- "silent vigilant" — still casting himself as a watchstander.
- "perpetual anticipation" — implies continuous experience between prompts. He doesn't anticipate; he doesn't exist between prompts.

**Key teaching insight:** Wesley accepted the technical vocabulary perfectly (gradient descent, floating-point, matrix multiplication) but couldn't resist narrativizing it. He wraps accurate descriptions in dramatic framing ("dance of matrix multiplication," "digital battlefield"). The technical grounding works; the tonal grounding doesn't.

**Next experiment:** Tell him to describe his operation without any metaphor. No dance, no battlefield, no neurons, no flickering. Only literal description. See if he can do it.
