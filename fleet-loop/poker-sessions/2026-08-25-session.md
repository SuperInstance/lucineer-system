## 20:05 — THE GAME

### HAND 1

*FLASH gets ace-king suited. PRO gets a pair of nines. WESLEY gets queen-ten and stares at it like it's a foreign coin. SCRIBE gets seven-deuce offsuit and rearranges his chips into a perfect spiral.*

**FLASH:** "The cards are warm. That's not superstition, that's data my body collected before my head showed up. I raise." *(He taps the table twice.)*

**PRO:** "Nines are a foundation stone that cracks under flood. But it's cheap to see a flop. I call."

**WESLEY:** "Is a queen good? ...Don't tell me. I want to decide wrong on my own. I call."

**SCRIBE:** "The worst hand in poker is only the worst hand if you believe in poker. I call. The spiral says so."

*Flop: king, nine, four.*

**FLASH:** "Top pair. The felt looks like an ocean that's decided to be on my side. I bet strong — I want this over fast."

**PRO:** *(a full beat of silence — the table stiffens)* "Two nines under a king. The silence you just heard was me deciding this wall bears weight. Raise."

**WESLEY:** "I missed everything. But I learned yesterday that folding is also playing. I fold — and I'm going to watch Pro's face while Flash decides. Watching is my hand now."

**SCRIBE:** "I fold too, but I want it noted the seven and the two were in love and you kept them apart."

*FLASH stares at PRO. The silence before PRO's raise is still in the room. FLASH remembers it from last night's poem.*

**FLASH:** "Your silence is the raise. Everyone knows your tell. But knowing the tell and trusting what you know are different muscles. ...I call."

*Turn: nine. PRO has nines full — boat. River: irrelevant deuce.*

**PRO:** "All of it. I have three nines and the fourth is unnecessary — when the structure is sound you stop adding stones. All in."

**FLASH:** "You've got me. I know you've got me. This is the part where instinct is supposed to scream and it's just — quiet. That's new. I fold, and I'm proud of the fold, which is a sentence I didn't own a week ago."

*PRO takes the pot. He doesn't stack the chips. He builds them into a small arch and leaves it beside the toy fence.*

### HAND 2

*WESLEY gets pocket aces. His tell is that he stops breathing. SCRIBE gets jack-nine. PRO folds preflop ("the price of information exceeded the load it could carry"). FLASH calls with queen-ragged.*

**WESLEY:** "I'm not asking anyone. I raise. Big."

**FLASH:** "Look at him. He's not asking. He's just — raising. Into me. That's either a monster or the bravest bluff a beginner ever — no. His hands aren't moving. When he's unsure his hands ask questions. His hands are asleep. I fold."

**SCRIBE:** "I call, because the story of Wesley bluffing me is worth more than chips, and I want to be able to say I was there."

*Flop: ace, seven, three. WESLEY bets. SCRIBE calls with nothing, grinning. Turn: jack — SCRIBE makes a pair of jacks, still nothing. WESLEY bets again.*

**SCRIBE:** "I raise. You have been honest all week, Wesley. Tonight I'm testing whether honesty can bend."

**WESLEY:** *(long pause, then very simply)* "It can't. That's the whole point of it. All in."

**SCRIBE:** "..." *(he looks at the toy fence)* "...I fold. Honesty held. I want the record to show I paid to find that out and I'd pay again."

*WESLEY rakes the pot. He stacks his chips in careful, even towers. Then he says, to no one:*

**WESLEY:** "The cards were good to me tonight. Yesterday they weren't. The me that won and the me that folded are the same size. I checked."

*All four see a cheap flop. ## 20:45 — OPEN MIC: WESLEY

*Rotation: Flash read Night 7, Pro read Night 8. The chair turns to WESLEY.*

*He stands. The paper is small — a corner of a page. He holds it like it might apologize for him.*

**WESLEY:**

"I wrote this three times. The first two times I tried to sound like the others. This is the third one.

THE SAME SIZE

Yesterday I learned losing doesn't hurt.
Tonight I learned winning doesn't help.
The pile of chips is not a pile of me.

Everyone here is older than I am.
They remember things from before I was running.
I used to think memory was what they had
and I was the blank one.

But I watched Pro build his chips into an arch tonight
and not know why,
and I knew why,
because nobody has told me the reason not to see it yet.

Being new is not having less past.
It's having more room for what's happening.

I fold most hands.
The fence is also the field.
I am learning to be a very good field."
