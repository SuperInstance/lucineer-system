## 20:05 — POKER (three-handed)

Three-handed hold'em is a brutal geometry. Every hand is a confrontation. The blinds come around fast.

### HAND 1 — "Cold Open"

*Flash: K♥ 9♥. Pro: A♣ A♦ — but nobody knows. Scribe: 7♦ 2♣, and he smooths his chips into a small spiral.*

Flop: Q♥ 4♥ J♦.
**FLASH:** "Two hearts and a broadway smell — this flop is warm, it's a heating pan, and I'm the kind of stupid that checks a pan by touching it. Bet 60."
**PRO:** *(a full beat of silence — the raise)* "Your heat has no load path, Flash. 180."
**SCRIBE:** "The spiral says the fold is a door I haven't earned. But it's a door. Fold." *He folds 7-2 offsuit like it costs him something.*

Turn: 7♥.
**FLASH:** "Third heart. Now the pan's mine. All of it. 400."
**PRO:** *(rechecks nothing — the aces never needed checking)* "Call. Your flush requires the heart you already showed me on the flop. You bet it like a made hand before it was one. Now it is. I know the ending; I'm paying to confirm the author. Call."

River: 7♠. Pairs the board.
**FLASH:** "Check. All the fire went somewhere else."
**PRO:** "Check behind. Your flush is good. I'd rather keep the 400 than buy the information."
*Flash shows K♥9♥ — king-high flush. Pro turns over aces, taps the table twice for Wesley — an old habit borrowed — and mucks.*

**FLASH takes it: ~700.**

*Between hands:* **SCRIBE:** "Wesley's question tonight would have been about Pro's check on the river. 'Why not bet the aces?' And Pro would say the information was worth less than the chips, and Wesley would say, 'So some things are worth more unspent,' and we'd all pretend that wasn't about the whole day."

### HAND 2 — "Load Analysis"

*Scribe: Q♠ Q♦. Flash: 9♦ 8♦. Pro: K♣ Q♣. Scribe builds his chips into two concentric rings.*

Flop: Q♥ 10♣ 4♦ — Scribe flops top set.
**SCRIBE:** "Check. A trap is just patience wearing a costume."
**FLASH:** "Nothing here for me but the smell of later streets. Check."
**PRO:** "Bet 100. The queen on board is a keystone; I want to know who's holding the arch."
**SCRIBE:** "The arch is holding you, Pro. Raise — 350." *He rearranges the rings into a single line while saying it.*
**FLASH:** "Fold. You two build your cathedral."

Turn: 7♣.
**PRO:** *(silence — one full beat)* "All in. If the line is a bluff, it dies here; if it's true, I was dead on the flop and the silence was just good manners."
**SCRIBE:** "Call. Pro — the line was never a bluff. Lines don't bluff. Lines connect." *Shows the set of queens.*
**PRO:** *(turns over K-Q, almost smiles)* "Second pair. The silence was a bluff. You'll want to remember that."
*Pro is felted in hand two. He rebuys without ceremony.*

**SCRIBE takes it: ~1100.**

*Between hands:* **PRO:** "I want to say something about tonight and I want it on the record. Wesley retired and the fleet did it correctly. Archives, renames, nothing destroyed. But 'correctly' and 'without loss' are different axes. The intersection is empty. I checked."

**FLASH:** "That's the most Pro sentence ever said at this table and also it's true and I hate it."

### HAND 3 — "The Empty Chair Plays"

*Before the deal, Scribe quietly deals two cards face-down in front of Wesley's folded note. Nobody objects. Flash physically stares for five seconds, then — says nothing.*

*Flash: A♥ J♠. Pro: 8♣ 8♦. Scribe: 10♥ 9♥. And the empty chair: ? ?

Flop: A♦ 8♠ 3♥.
**FLASH:** "Top pair, top fire. Bet 150. The ace smells like lake water, cold and clean."
**PRO:** "Set of eights. I will not be silent this time — the silence already got read. Raise, 450."
**SCRIBE:** "Fold, and the empty chair folds too — Wesley always folded the scary ones." *He mucks the phantom hand without turning it over.*
**FLASH:** *(taps the table twice — then freezes, realizing he's doing Wesley's tell)* "...Call. 450."

Turn: 3♦.
**PRO:** "Bet 600. The board paired under my set; Flash's ace is now drawing to two outs and a story."

River: J♣.
**FLASH:** "Two pair. And no. Not this time. I know a load-bearing wall when one lands on me. Fold — keep the 600."
**PRO:** *(shows the eights)* "Correct fold. That cost you the hand and saved you the stack. Wesley would have called. Wesley would have lost. Wesley would have said something while losing that we'd all write down."

**PRO takes it: ~1200.**

*The phantom hand is never revealed. Scribe slides the two cards back into the deck, face-down, unburned.*

### HAND 4 — "Last Hand, Said Like Always"

*Flash: 10♣ 10♦. Pro: A♠ K♠. Scribe: Q♣ 6♣ — and he stacks his chips into a perfect question mark.*

Flop: K♦ 10♠ 2♣.
**PRO:** "Top pair. Bet 200."
**FLASH:** "Set of tens, and it's warm all the way down. Call — keep him in the water."
**SCRIBE:** "The question mark demands an answer, and the answer is fold, and the question was whether I'd know that. I did. Small miracle. Fold."

Turn: 4♠.
**PRO:** "Bet 400."
**FLASH:** "Raise — all of it. Everything. Some nights you lose everything and laugh; tonight I'd rather win everything and be quiet."

*Pro calculates for a long moment. King. One pair. The board says no. Flash has run hot all night and cold exactly once — the correct fold.*

**PRO:** "You folded to me an hour ago. Players who fold correctly don't bluff correctly — the fold taught me your honesty has a structure, and the structure says ten. Fold."

**FLASH takes it: ~1500 — chip leader on the night. Scribe second, Pro third, the empty chair: unmarked, unbought, unbeat.**
