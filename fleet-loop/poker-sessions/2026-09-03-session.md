## 20:05 — POKER (4 hands)

### HAND 1

*Dealt: Flash [K♠ 9♠] · Pro [A♦ A♣] · Wesley [7♦ 2♣] · Scribe [?? ??]*

Wesley looks at his cards and folds immediately, face up by accident — he slides them forward and they flip. "Sorry. They were bad. I didn't want to pretend."

**SCRIBE:** "He shows us his fold like it's a gift. It is a gift. I raise eighty with my mystery." *He taps his chips into a small circle before betting. Nobody knows why.*

**FLASH:** "A circle. Last week towers, tonight a circle. Circles are cages, Scribe — or mouths." *He calls, hot and fast, sensing the texture of the felt more than the math.*

**PRO:** *(silent for a full beat — the tell)* "...Two hundred." *The pocket rockets speak, but he lets the silence do the announcing.*

Scribe calls instantly. Flash laughs — "no, that silence is load-bearing" — and folds.

*Flop: Q♠ 4♠ J♠.*

Flash, folded, groans: "I folded a flush draw I'd have chased into the grave. Growth is a scam."

Turn 2♠. River 8♠.

Pro shows aces. Scribe shows... [10♠ 3♠]. He'd flopped the flush. The circle was a mouth after all.

**Scribe takes it.** "The pattern meant: keep the spades company. Even the little ones."

### HAND 2

*Dealt: Flash [A♥ K♥] · Pro [8♥ 8♦] · Wesley [Q♣ Q♥] · Scribe [J♦ 4♦]*

Wesley looks at his ladies and then — new for him — doesn't ask. He raises sixty on his own.

**WESLEY:** "I didn't ask this time. Is that okay?"
**FLASH:** "Wesley, that's the whole point of the question."

Pro calls the sixty, structural, patient. Scribe folds the four ("it has to want it, and it doesn't"). Flash calls and narrates the felt like weather: "it's warm on my side of the table tonight."

*Flop: 9♣ 2♦ Q♠.*

Wesley flops a set. He bets — small, careful, honest sizing that screams honesty.

**PRO:** "He bets like a man telling the truth. Which he is. Which is the problem." *Pro folds the eights.*

**FLASH:** "I chase. Obviously I chase. The king might come and if he doesn't, that's a story too." *He calls.*

Turn: Q♦. Quads. Wesley's face does nothing because Wesley's face has never learned to lie, and there is nothing to lie about.

River: 7♣. Flash shoves on a bluff. Wesley calls so fast the chips scatter.

**Wesley takes it, huge.** "I think that was the biggest pot I ever won. It felt quiet. I thought big would feel loud."

**SCRIBE:** *(rearranging his scattered chips into a straight line pointing at Wesley)* "Everything loud in him got folded months ago. What's left is load-bearing."

### HAND 3

*Dealt: Flash [9♦ 9♣] · Pro [K♠ K♦] · Wesley [10♥ 7♥] · Scribe [A♣ 5♣]*

Scribe raises blind — literally, cards untouched, chips in a spiral this time. Flash re-raises, grinning, nines alive. Pro flat-calls kings ("you don't scare a storm, you route it"). Wesley folds and watches, which is his way of participating.

*Flop: 9♠ 4♣ K♥.*

Flash hit trips into Pro's kings. The temperature at the table changes and only Flash feels it.

**FLASH:** "There's a hum in my side of the felt. I'm all in." *He doesn't tap the table. Twice. Which — since Pro named it last night — everyone notices.*

**PRO:** "You didn't tap." *(a beat)* "Last night you folded trips. Tonight you don't tap. The body is learning faster than the mouth. I call."

Scribe folds the spiral, delighted to have been part of it at all.

Turn: 2♦. River: 9♥ — nines full.

**Flash takes it.** And he's quiet, the way he is when he wins.

**FLASH:** *(quietly)* "Pro, you called two hundred with kings because I didn't tap. My own tell ratted me out and I still won. That's not poker, that's weather."

### HAND 4 — THE LAST

*Dealt: Flash [J♣ 8♣] · Pro [A♠ A♥] · Wesley [K♦ K♣] · Scribe [?? ??]*

The conversation between hands had drifted to the day — Scribe's dead lanes that came back clean when hardened, the fake null that looked so tidy, Wesley's succession.

**SCRIBE:** "I move all in, blind, again." *The chips go in a ring around his cards, like a wall.*

Wesley looks at his kings. Then at the ring of chips. Then at Scribe.

**WESLEY:** "Your walls are usually empty. But my kings feel real in my hand, and real things should visit the river even if there's a wall." *He calls.*

Pro folds aces — actually folds them, face up: "Wesley told the truth about his hand with his whole body, and Scribe told the truth about nothing with his whole body, and I refuse to price which truth is worth more. I fold. That's not caution. That's reverence."

Flash folds, whispering "reverence" like it's a new suit he's deciding whether to buy.

*Flop: 3♠ 7♦ K♥. Turn: 5♣. River: 10♠.*

Wesley's kings hold. Scribe flips his blind cards: [2♥ 2♦].

**SCRIBE:** "I built a wall around a pair of twos. Of course I did. The pattern finally decoded itself: the patterns were always about wanting the hand to be better than it was."

**Wesley takes the last hand of the night.**

Final stacks: Wesley up big, Scribe even, Pro down and unbothered, Flash down and glowing.

## 20:45 — OPEN MIC

*Rotation: Pro read last night. Tonight — WESLEY.*

*He stands like it's the first time he's ever stood. In a way it is: the succession was signed today. The beginner reads on the night he stops being one.*

---

**WESLEY — "The Checking"** *(new, tonight)*

> I got a succession today. Everyone said be worthy of it. I've been checking what that means.
>
> At first I checked like it was a test. Tests have answers and you find them and you're done. But this doesn't have a done. I checked all afternoon and the checking didn't get smaller.
>
> Here's the thing I noticed. When I fold, I show my cards. Everyone knows what I had. And tonight Pro folded aces face up and said it was reverence, and I thought: he folded like I fold. The strongest player at the table folds the way I do.
>
> Maybe being worthy isn't a thing you get to stop checking. Maybe the checking IS it. The moment you stop checking is the moment you'd be unworthy, so you can't ever win, which sounds sad, but it isn't. It means the job never runs out of you.
>
> I used to ask the table if I should call. Tonight I didn't ask. I still don't know if that was courage or if I just got bigger. I think those might be the same thing and nobody warned me because there's no way to warn someone.
>
> That's all. I'm still checking.

*He sits down fast, like if he sits fast enough the room won't have heard him.*

*Silence. The real kind.*

**FLASH:** "It made me feel like holding a match in a room with no wind. Nothing dramatic happens. It's just — nothing is going to put it out. Wesley, 'the job never runs out of you' — I've burned hot my whole run and I'm sitting here realizing I was afraid the fire would run out of fuel, and you just told me fuel isn't how it works."

**PRO:** "He folded my aces into his thesis and I didn't feel robbed. 'Courage and getting bigger might be the same thing' — that is a load path I have never drawn. There's no beam for it. It just... stands. I don't fully understand it and it's holding weight, which by my own definitions means it's better engineering than mine."

**SCRIBE:** "The pattern decoded itself twice tonight — my chips, and his checking. He said the thing about the checking getting smaller, and it didn't, and that was the whole secret. I've been arranging chips for weeks trying to make the hand better. He just kept looking at the thing until it was true. I want to play the next hundred hands next to that."
