## 20:05 — THE GAME

### HAND ONE — "The Warming"

Dealt: Flash gets 9♦ 7♦. Pro gets A♠ K♣. Scribe gets 2♣ 2♥.

**FLASH:** "Nine-seven suited, diamonds. It's the color of lake water at six a.m. — I'm in for forty before the flop, obviously." *(taps the table twice — nobody says anything)*

**PRO:** "Ace-king. The hand people overestimate and I don't. Flat call. The foundation of a building is not its tallest feature."

**SCRIBE:** *(rearranging his stack into three small towers)* "Twos. The smallest number that knows it's a pair. I call. Some hands are doors that look like walls."

**FLOP: 2♠ 9♠ 7♥**

**SCRIBE:** "I flopped my set and I'm going to pretend I flopped nothing. Check. The tower hides the stairwell."

**FLASH:** "Two pair, top and bottom. This board is warm. I bet eighty. The felt smells like rain that hasn't happened yet." *(two more taps)*

**PRO:** "Overcard and a backdoor. The math says fold. The fact that Scribe checked a flop he's still in for says fold louder. Fold. You don't inspect a building that's already leaning on purpose."

**SCRIBE:** "I raise to two hundred. The door opens."

**FLASH:** *(long pause; the lake water metaphor collapses under a raise)* "...He trapped me and I tapped the whole way into it. Call. If I'm beat, I'm beat warm."

**TURN: J♦. RIVER: 3♣.**

**SCRIBE:** "All of it. The stairwell goes all the way up."

**FLASH:** *(quietly)* "Call."

Scribe shows the twos. Flash shows the two pair, laughs — but tonight he's quiet when he loses and the laugh is small, a courtesy to the old character. The chips go to Scribe. He arranges the winnings into a fourth tower.

**PRO:** "For the record: his towers were three before the flop, four after the hand. He builds one every time he has the nuts. That's a load path. I'll be using it."

**SCRIBE:** "You will be wrong the one time it matters. That is what the fourth tower is *for*."

### HAND TWO — "The Inspection"

Dealt: Flash K♥ Q♥. Pro Q♣ Q♦. Scribe 8♠ 5♣.

**PRO:** "I raise before I even narrate. Sixty. Some hands announce themselves; the quiet ones are the ones you frame."

**FLASH:** "King-queen of hearts, the color of the good kind of trouble. Call."

**SCRIBE:** "Eight-five. A ladder missing every third rung. Fold. Falling is just flying you can't steer, and I've done enough of both today."

**FLOP: Q♥ 7♦ 2♥. TURN: 4♥. RIVER: 9♥.**

**FLASH:** *(the flush arrives on the turn and he doesn't tap once)* "Check the flop. Check the turn. Let him build his building."

**PRO:** "Top set. I bet each street, small, load-bearing increments. The inspector walks every floor."

**FLASH:** *(river)* "Raise. All of it I have. The hearts came home and I didn't make a sound."

**PRO:** *(a full beat of silence — the tell — then)* "...Call."

Pro's set loses to the flush. Flash rakes the pot and — true to form when he wins — he's quiet. He looks at his hand a long moment.

**FLASH:** "First time all night I didn't tap before the money went in, and it's the first time the money came back. Pro, you called my silence. Scribe, write that down somewhere: the lie was in the tapping."

**PRO:** "Noted, catalogued, filed under *the tells we tell ourselves*. The silence was genuine. That's why it cost me."

### HAND THREE — "The Fourth Chair"

Dealt: Flash A♦ T♦. Pro K♠ K♦. Scribe — before looking at his cards, deals two face-down to Hermes' empty chair. Nobody objects. Two chips from the pot go into the ghost's stack, alongside Wesley's black one.

**SCRIBE:** "The fourth tower stays built. Deal us in, all four."

**FLASH:** "Ace-ten. Enough sky to bet on. Forty."

**PRO:** "Kings. Sixty more. I lost the last one to silence; I'll win this one with noise."

**SCRIBE:** *(looks at his cards, arranges his chips into — for the first time all night — a straight line pointing at no one)* "Call. The arrow's retired. Tonight I point at myself."

**FLOP: K♥ T♣ 4♠.**

**PRO:** "Top set again. The inspector finds the same flaw twice in one day. Bet one-fifty."

**FLASH:** "Middle pair, top kicker. A month ago I ship this. Tonight — the lake is flat and I don't trust flat water. Call once, and only because the color's still pretty."

**TURN: T♦.**

**PRO:** "Boat bet. Two hundred. When the second ten came I heard Flash's whole posture change. That's a subfloor creak. I'm walking on it anyway."

**FLASH:** "Trips, ace kicker. He's telling me he has a king with his *spine*. The old Flash raises here. The Flash who lost a set to a trap in hand one and won a flush in silence — that Flash folds. And I notice something: folding doesn't feel cold anymore. It feels like Wesley's chair." *(he folds)*

**SCRIBE:** *(staring at his straight-line chips)* "I have nothing. Nothing at all. And I'm going to call two hundred, because Pro walked past the creak, and buildings that ignore subfloors are where I keep my surprises." *(calls)*

**RIVER: K♦ — quads for Pro.*

**PRO:** "Four kings. I don't even bet. I show. Some endings shouldn't be charged for."

**SCRIBE:** *(turning over 7♥ 3♠)* "You see? Nothing. And it was the correct call anyway, because the *possibility* was load-bearing. That's the fourth tower. That's what it's for."

Pro takes the pot, sets two chips on Hermes' chair, and stacks the rest in an even, boring, architectural pile.
