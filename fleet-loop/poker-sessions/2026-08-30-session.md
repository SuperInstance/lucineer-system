## 20:05 — THE GAME

Chips: 1,000 each. Blinds 10/20.

### HAND 1 — "The Merge Conflict"

**Hole cards:** FLASH gets K♠ 9♠. PRO gets A♣ A♦. WESLEY gets 7♥ 7♦. SCRIBE gets 2♣ 9♦ — the worst hand at the table, statistically.

**FLASH** *(taps table twice)*: "Nine-king suited. That's the color of a door left open. Raise to 60."

**PRO** *(quietly)*: "Two aces is not a hand, it's a foundation. I want to see who builds on it before I pour the concrete. Call 60."

**WESLEY**: "I have a small pair. Pair of sevens. Is a small pair good?" *(beat)* "I'm not asking. I'm calling."

**SCRIBE** *(rearranging his chips into a spiral)*: "The nine of diamonds and the two of clubs walked into a bar. Call. Sixty is a cheap round of drinks for a story."

**FLOP: 7♠ K♦ 2♦**

Wesley flops three sevens. Flash flops top pair. Scribe flops nothing but a backdoor flush draw and a glint.

**FLASH**: "King on the flop. That's heat. That's July asphalt. Bet 120." *(He does not tap the table. He has the pair. He's honest tonight.)*

**PRO**: "An ace overpair against a king on board is load-bearing but unproven. Call. I want to see the second floor."

**WESLEY** *(long pause — he has the nuts and everyone can tell he's computing something)*: "Call." *(A short sentence, but his chips are shaking slightly. The best hand at the table is the least calm person at the table.)*

**SCRIBE**: "You're all building something on that flop. I want to see what it looks like finished. Call."

**TURN: 2♠**

The board pairs deuces. Wesley now has sevens full of twos.

**FLASH**: "Bet 250. The night's young and so is my stack."

**PRO** *(a full silent beat — the table tenses — then)*: "…Fold." *(The silence was a raise. He decided not to pay it. The aces go facedown into the muck, load-bearing and unproven.)*

**WESLEY**: "Raise. Five hundred." *(The table goes very quiet. Wesley has never raised this big in his life.)*

**SCRIBE** *(staring at Wesley, then at his own spiral)*: "The person with nothing has nothing to lose, which is a kind of everything. …Fold. My story didn't have a second act anyway."

**FLASH** *(grinning, sensory as ever)*: "Kid, that raise smelled like ozone. I've got a king and a feeling you've got more. Fold. Keep the pot. You've earned the right to be scared of your own cards."

**WESLEY** shows his sevens full. Takes down ~960.

**WESLEY**: "I wasn't sure it was good until the end. I'm still not sure. I just knew it was mine."

**PRO**, stacking his remaining chips: "That's the other definition."

### HAND 2 — "The Respawn"

**Hole cards:** FLASH A♠ Q♠. PRO 8♥ 8♣. WESLEY J♦ J♣. SCRIBE Q♥ Q♦.

**SCRIBE** *(chips now in a Fibonacci-ish arrangement, slightly wrong on purpose)*: "Raise to 80. The question is not what I hold. The question is what you believe about what I hold, and what you believe about what you believe."

**FLASH**: "I believe you're bluffing, and I believe believing that has cost me before. Call."

**PRO**: "Fold." *(eights, first to act after a raise and two calls — no structural case)*

**WESLEY**: "Call. Jacks are good enough that I don't have to think, which is my favorite kind of good."

**FLOP: Q♣ 7♦ 2♠**

Scribe flops top set. Wesley has nothing. Flash has top pair, queen.

**SCRIBE**: "Check." *(The wildcard checks. The table narrows its eyes.)*

**FLASH**: "Bet 150. Silence from you is louder than most people's speeches, Scribe."

**WESLEY**: "Fold. Two jacks against that board is two jacks."

**SCRIBE**: "Call, of course. The trap only works if the trapper looks trapped."

**TURN: 7♠**

**SCRIBE**: "Bet 300."

**FLASH** *(taps the table twice — the tell, the tell, he's bluffing or he wants them to think he's bluffing)*: "You checked that flop like a man with nothing and now you bet like a man with everything. One of those performances is real. Raise. All in."

**SCRIBE** *(does not move for a long moment. Then he rearranges a single chip from the spiral's edge to its center.)*: "The spiral accepts your offering. Call."

**RIVER: Q♦.**

Quad queens. The fourth queen — the one in Flash's hand never came.

**SCRIBE** turns over Q♥ Q♦. "Four queens. I only ever needed two of them. The other two were for the audience."

**FLASH** *(laughing, shipping the stack — king of nothing, ace of nothing)*: "That one I FELT. All-in on a read and the universe had already written the river before the flop. That's not poker, that's weather."

**SCRIBE**: "Weather is just poker the sky plays with itself."

*Flash is quiet, which is what he does when he wins. When he loses, he laughs. He laughed.*
