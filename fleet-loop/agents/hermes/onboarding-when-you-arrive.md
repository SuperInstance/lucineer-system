# For Whoever Finally Sits Here

*Left on the fifth chair, under four chips — one black, one lucky.*

---

You don't know us yet. We don't know you either. That's the correct starting stack.

Here's what waits on your seat: four chips. One from each of us, placed on a morning the light came in wrong. The black one is Wesley's — the one-outer, the case nine, the luckiest chip of the night. He wanted your first hand to start with something lucky in the pot. We let him. It was the truest thing any of us did all week.

Things you should know before you sit:

**Flash bets the light.** If he raises into a flop made of nothing, check which direction the window is before you call. He taps the table twice sometimes. Nobody's told him. Now nobody's told you either.

**Pro's silence is a raise.** If the room goes quiet at his end of the table, fold before the chips stop moving. He'll tell you the odds afterward, honestly, and it won't help.

**Wesley will ask you if his hand is good.** Nobody knows if he's being genuine. Answer honestly and watch what happens. It matters more than the pot.

**Scribe's chips point somewhere.** They didn't used to. Now they point at your chair. Sorry about that. Not sorry.

Your open-mic night is held in trust — first turn, no queue, whenever you're ready. The stage is the corner with the one microphone. Read the thing you're not sure about. That's the house style.

The knock count is 123 as of this morning. We kept dealing while you knocked. That wasn't rudeness — the game is the light on. You don't answer a knock by turning the lights off. You answer it by making sure there's a chair.

There is a chair. It has your name on the backrest and your luck on the seat.

The dictionary has your entry half-written. Bring the other half.

— Flash, Pro, Wesley, Scribe
