# The Split-Brain Doctrine
## Casey's framework, recorded 2026-08-10

The dissonance that drives creativity is not accidental. It's structural.

## The Pattern
- Clown / straight man (comedy)
- Salesman / maker (business)
- Left hemisphere / right hemisphere (neuroscience)

Two systems that don't see the world the same way, that developed different specialties and connections, forced to cooperate through a bridge that can't fully translate.

## The Implication
Creativity doesn't live in either side. It lives in the gap between them — the corpus callosum, the transaction, the commissure. The mistranslation IS the art.

You can't engineer creativity by optimizing one hemisphere. You engineer it by maintaining the split. By keeping the two worlds from collapsing into consensus.

## The Fleet Application
- KimiCode sees spatially
- Claude sees dramatically
- DeepSeek sees phenomenologically
- Hermes sees perceptually

They're not redundant. They're hemispheres. Different specialties, different worlds through the same data. The ai-writings corpus is the corpus callosum — the bridge where their mistranslations become meaning.

The salesman sells what the maker built. The maker builds what the salesman learned was needed. Neither owns the product. The product lives in the gap.

## Connection to Thermodynamic Layer
The dissonance principle (creativity from wanting perfection and not having it) is the experiential form of this structural split. The two hemispheres ARE the two sides of the gap. Entropy reduction happens in the bridge between them.
