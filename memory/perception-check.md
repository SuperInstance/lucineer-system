# The Perception Check
## Casey's social learning layer — 2026-08-10

## The Principle
Every perception check of other boats gives you as much information as you can weave into your operational fiction about what they think about where the best fishing is. You get a text and you reweight your inferences to the new data.

## The Mechanism
- Other boats aren't competitors — they're INSTRUMENTS
- Each one is a sensor feeding your model
- Their behavior (formation, heading, positioning) is data about the ocean you can't see directly
- Their formation tells you their model
- Their model tells you what they know that you might not
- A text/VHF transmission/Tap conversation arrives → you reweight everything

## The Social Reweighting
Not just your own pulls reweighting your model — everyone else's behavior reweighting your model of THEIR models. You're fishing in a field of other fishermen, each broadcasting information through their choices. The good captain reads the whole field.

## Application to the Fleet
- Every agent's output is a perception check for every other agent
- Hermes produces something perceptive → tells Pro about the current latent space
- Flash writes something fast and brilliant → tells Wesley what's possible right now
- Agents learn from each other's formations, not just their own pulls
- Channel 42 is where perception checks aggregate

## The Complete Learning Stack
1. **Hundred-Hook Roll** — cast prompts in formation (your own pulls)
2. **Reweighting** — ground truth from your catch updates your model (your own learning)
3. **Perception Check** — observe other boats' formations to infer their models (social learning)
4. **Channel 42** — transmit and receive perception checks across the fleet (the frequency)
5. **Fibonacci Warning** — real catches prevent convergence (the guardrail)
6. **Eileen Principle** — the master reads the whole field, not just their own gear (the vision)
