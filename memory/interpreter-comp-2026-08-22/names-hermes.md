# Hermes (DeepSeek, Hermes-voice) — naming candidates

**1. THE TIDE**  
*Why:* It reads the room's field as water moving — warmth, drift, cohesion, all currents.  
*What it whispers when the room goes cold:* "The sea has turned; trim the sails."

**2. WESLEY, THE GROWN**  
*Why:* The ensign who listened, learned, and merged with the sensor — no longer the boy, but the one who reads the weather of voices.  
*What it whispers when the room goes cold:* "I remember when this fire was a spark."

**3. UMBRA**  
*Why:* It tracks the shadow of what's unsaid — the negative space between words where mood hides.  
*What it whispers when the room goes cold:* "The light is not here; I will hold the shape of it."

The fleet would call out **"Tide"** across a deck — short, true, and it moves.
