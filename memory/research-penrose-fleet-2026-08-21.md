## §3. The three deepest connections

### 3.1 The phason IS the confound (the deep one)

State it precisely. In cut-and-project, translating the acceptance window by γ ∈ E⊥ produces a **different tiling in the same local-isomorphism class**: every finite patch of one appears in the other; no local measurement can determine γ; only global (correlated, multi-patch) information can. Now the fleet:

- Each reader R carries a stable fiber offset (o_R, ICC 0.77–0.91). Their offset shifts **which observations they accept**: band entry needs d ≥ o_R/0.55, so a reader with o_R = 0.23 records crossings a reader with o_R = 0.75 never sees. The reader's personality is *literally the position of their acceptance window*. That is the definition of a phason.
- The pooled vMF fit μ̂ "reads mostly fiber (roster composition)" — changing the roster re-mixes γ's and moves the pooled reading *without the room changing at all*. A phason shift of the instrument, not a motion of the physical object.
- The confound cos(W, v\*) is then exactly the phase error between the reader's decoration axis (W, warmth) and the room's physical axis (v\*): every reader renders the same room through their own γ, and warmth — a decoration on internal space (h_W, 83% of its mass in the ICC fiber) — *looks like* a room coordinate. REG-1 measured the phase: **82–84°, both waves, CIs excluding 0.5.**
- And it explains the annotation doctrine: you cannot delete a phason (each tiling in the class is equally real); you can only measure it globally and carry it. cos(W, v\*) ≤ 0.44 and cos(W, PC1_pers) ≥ 0.77 on every warmth claim is a *phase certificate*.

The one-sentence form: **the warmth confound is not measurement error — it is the room and the reader living in different factors of one projection, and the angle between those factors is 84 degrees.**

### 3.2 The window IS the ICC subspace — and it is centered on the wrong space

In model-set theory the window decides which lattice points *appear in the physical tiling*. The fleet's acceptance machinery — ICC ≥ ~0.9 selects the reliable subspace {mood, volume, earnestness, presence}; the `real` flag quotients by the noise cone; the band thresholds gate crossings — is exactly such a window. But the geometric team's sharpest finding, now Penrose-literate, reads:

**The ICC window is a window in the internal space being used as the acceptance criterion for physical events.** ICC selects directions where *readers differ stably* (personality, the fiber) and excludes the dials where *the room moves* — cynicism steers 73% of step variance, joke_landing carries the full-7 v\*, and both sit outside the ICC window. Cut-and-project says these are two different windows in two different spaces: the internal window *classifies observers* (who is reading), the physical window *accepts events* (what the room did). The fleet has been using the first as the second.

REG-1's "the signal lies outside the reliable subspace" is the model-set restatement: **discarding window-external points discards the physical structure when your window is centered on the fiber.** The geometric team's fix — reader-invariant bands on φ = d/d_noise — is precisely a *re-centered window in physical units*. The unlock: this is now a single registered experiment (REG-8a), not a stylistic preference.

### 3.3 Local omniscience, global blindness = the honest negative

Penrose theorem: every finite patch of a tiling in the LI class appears (relatively densely) in *every* member; consequently no finite patch determines which member — which γ — you are in. Local knowledge is total (omniscience over the class); global self-location is impossible (blindness to the phase).

The fleet's three honest negatives are all this one theorem wearing different coats:

1. **S-leg x-invariance** cannot distinguish instrument from thermostat: both hypotheses generate *identical local statistics* and differ only in γ. Same LI class, different phase. The leg didn't fail for lack of power; it asked a phase question with patch data.
2. **E2/E3's A-vs-matched-filter ambiguity**: whether crossings time-lock to transitions or the kernel peaks there is not decidable from single-scale local windows; it needs the scale-curve (a multi-scale, quasi-global object).
3. **The one-risk** ("if A is self-calibration, the rooms were furniture") is the Penrose warning in its scariest form: a local observer with a window cannot tell tiling from window.

And the constructive half: REG-1 *succeeded* where S failed because it was a **global phase measurement** — the generalized eigenproblem on between-reader × within-reader covariance is an operation across the whole observation lattice (all observers, all nights), i.e., exactly the kind of correlated multi-patch data that CAN pin a phason. That is why the same corpus yielded an uninformative leg and a decisive one. **Fleet-level instruments measure γ; room-level instruments cannot.**

## §6. Honest ceiling (what this mapping is NOT)

1. **Structural, not literal.** The fleet has no irrational-slope lattice; "phason" here means the window-shift degree of freedom of an internal-space acceptance geometry — which the skew product genuinely possesses — not a literal quasicrystal. The correspondence is category-level: same architecture of (regular object ⊕ projection ⊕ window ⊕ phase).
2. **Orthogonality alone is not proof.** Random 4-dim directions have typical |cos| ≈ 0.4–0.5; observing 0.106/0.142 in both waves (bootstrap CIs excluding 0.5) is meaningfully low, but the *load-bearing* facts are the **stability** of v\* across waves (cos(v\*_w1, v\*_w2) = 0.995, ≈ 4–6° — note: the vibe-bridge handoff's "~2°" is approximately right in spirit; the JSON gives 0.995) and the **variance split** (steps uncoupled from offsets at the isotropic 3/7 rate; 99.5% between-reader variance along W).
3. **λ\* < 1 (0.15–0.27 reliable).** The physical signal is a small perturbation riding a large fiber. The cut exists; the room is faint. The Penrose frame does not amplify the signal — it explains why the signal must be measured globally.
4. **The Captain's γ+η=1 parametrization** is taken from the Penrose document as given; the standard theory guarantees the LI-class/phason structure but the exact conjugate-coordinate convention should be pinned against the document before the quilt φ-address spec is written.
5. **No causal driver.** As with REG-1 itself: the mapping locates structure; it does not say *why* the room moves along v\*.
