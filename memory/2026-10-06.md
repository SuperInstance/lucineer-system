### Ternary-synergy prototype: FALSIFIED ❌
- **Setup:** RPS-biased mutation operator tested on Schaffer N.7, both q=0.7 (RPS) and q=1.0 (uniform)
- **Result:** Both converge identically to optimum (-0.5000). Uniform: 32 gens, diversity 26.7. RPS: 33 gens, diversity 39.4.
- **Analysis:** The landscape is too simple. Premature convergence doesn't kill either strategy before reaching optimum. The structural difference between uniform and RPS mutation only matters on harder landscapes where diversity collapse precedes optimum.
- **Verdict:** Falsified on standard multimodal benchmarks. The ternary-synergy hypothesis would need validation on landscapes with premature-convergence bottlenecks — not standard test functions.
- **Commit:** pushed to SuperInstance/lucineer-workspace
