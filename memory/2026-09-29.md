### The first principle (distilled from the whole night)
**A record's job is not to resemble its source. Its job is to serve the operations the fleet will perform on it: being found, being read, being related, being audited, being aged.** TC1–TC3 proved the first half (fidelity ≠ usefulness; objective > budget; sufficiency knee at 96B). The six broken encoders each contributed ONE face of the full answer.

### THE FIVE-FACE TILE (the synthesis — plato's 384 bytes, re-portioned)
Plato's layout serves ONE face (menu). TC2/TC3 served ONE face (meaning). First-principles design serves all five at the SAME 384-byte total:
1. **Meaning face (96B)** — retrieval-trained code (TC3 knee). How the tile is FOUND.
2. **Menu face (~200B)** — human/agent-readable fields (question/answer/domain). How the tile is READ. (Rack-flip doctrine at byte level: front = ActiveLog, back = ActiveLedger.)
3. **Relation face (implicit/cheap)** — delta from the room anchor, not an absolute code (delta-encode's idea, promoted from compression to SEMANTICS: a room is a predictive prior; deltas are evidence against the prior; the anchor is the room's name).
4. **Witness face (~8B)** — bech32-BCH checksum over the meaning code + human-readable id: tiles become SELF-VERIFYING without re-embedding the source; undetectable corruption becomes detectable (plato's silent total-loss is the pathological case). sha256 stays for content addressing; bech32-of-meaning is the audit id.
5. **State face (1.5B — the dodecet, counterintuitively)** — a 12-bit lifecycle word per event/tile: error level (4b ρ) ⊕ direction (4b) ⊕ basin/chamber (3b) ⊕ safety (1b). Not geometry — PROPRIOCEPTION: "how wrong am I, which way am I moving, which basin am I in, am I safe to act." 4096 states = a vocabulary of situations; a 1M-event audit tape costs 1.5MB; merkle over 12-bit words = the E1 receipt proposal, now grounded.
