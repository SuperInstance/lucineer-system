**Discovery:** Not all six Eisenstein unit directions are at hex_distance 1 from the origin. (1,1) has hex distance 2 despite norm 1. The Eisenstein norm and the hex distance are *different metrics* — norm measures algebraic size, hex distance measures grid topology. This is a real mathematical fact, not a bug.
