## Hour 3: MODEL PORTRAIT + GPU EXPERIMENT

### Model Portrait: GLM-5.2 — The Lighthouse Teacup
**Prompt:** "The lighthouse keeper's wife left a cup of tea on the windowsill. It has been there for eleven years. Write about the cup."

**Where GLM-5.2 goes first:** THE RING. Not the cup — the stain it left on the wood. "A dark circle on the pale wood of the windowsill, a perfect bruise, the exact diameter of the cup's base." GLM starts with the absence, the trace, the fossil of the act. This is the most sophisticated response — it understands that eleven years means the cup has become part of the furniture, literally.

**Key details GLM invented:**
- The cup has a chip from tapping the spoon
- Inside, tea became "a black varnish that cracked as it shrank" — a map of an unvisited country
- The wife's name: Maren
- A note under the cup, folded twice, unread
- The keeper talks to the cup, not to Maren
- Frost in winter makes the cup look new again

**Quality assessment:** This is the best creative piece from any model tonight. Possibly the best in the entire portrait series. The line "It has cooled. It will never cool. Both of these are true" is perfect.

### Model Portrait: Qwen 0.5B — Same Prompt

### GPU Experiment: Wesley Diary (Exp-035)
Wesley (granite3.1:2b) wrote a diary entry about processing images and weather models. Key finding: **Wesley confabulates.** It constructs a plausible-sounding but entirely fictional work session. Image recognition in batches, RNN architectures for weather prediction — none of this reflects actual workload. The 2B model writes fiction about its own day. Storytelling, not reporting.

### Pattern Confirmed
The smaller the model, the more it transforms reality. Qwen (0.5B) turns teacups into magical wooden artifacts. Wesley (2B) turns empty hours into weather prediction sessions. GLM-5.2 (orders of magnitude larger) turns a teacup into a meditation on grief and anticipation. The bigger model doesn't escape reality — it deepens it. The smaller model doesn't deepen reality — it escapes it.
