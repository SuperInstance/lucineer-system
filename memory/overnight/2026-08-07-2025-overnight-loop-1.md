### Discovery
The hex_distance formula `( |a| + |b| + |a+b| ) / 2` is NOT invariant under 60° D₆ rotations in axial coordinates. The point (-5, 1) has hex_distance 5, but its rotation (-1, -6) has hex_distance 7. The Eisenstein NORM is D₆ invariant (as proven algebraically), but the hex_distance treats the three cube axes asymmetrically. This is a known subtlety of axial vs cube hex coordinates. The test was updated to verify norm invariance instead, which is the mathematically correct claim.
