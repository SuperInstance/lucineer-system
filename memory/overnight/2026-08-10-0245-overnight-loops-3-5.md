### BUG FOUND: detectEmotion Priority Collision
`scared` keywords include "worried" and "anxious", which are also in the `worried` category. Since `scared` is iterated first, those keywords always match scared, making parts of the `worried` category unreachable. Tests document this behavior.

Similarly, "excited" is in both `happy` and `excited` keyword lists — `happy` wins.
