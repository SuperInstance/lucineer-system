### BUG FOUND: negative radius in findRoomsNear
**Severity:** Low
**Description:** `findRoomsNear(point, -10)` treats negative radius as positive because `r2 = radius * radius`. A -10 radius searches within 10 units instead of returning empty.
**Documented in test.**

### BUG FOUND: registerRoom doesn't index portals in roomPortals
**Severity:** Medium
**Description:** When `registerRoom()` is called individually (not via `registerWorld()`), the room's exits are stored in `this.portals` but NOT in `this.roomPortals`. This means `getPortalsFrom()` returns empty for these rooms, and pathfinding doesn't work. You must use `createPortal()` separately, or register via `registerWorld()`.
**Workaround in tests:** Used `createPortal()` instead of relying on room.exits.
