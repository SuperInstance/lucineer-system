### Negative Space Finding
The dodecahedron/cube shared-output pattern is the morning's best discovery. The PlatonicRNG algorithm XORs vertex coordinates into the PRNG state, but when two solids share vertices with identical coordinates (±1 corners), the XOR contribution is identical. The solids only diverge when their vertex cycles reach unique coordinates. This means the "texture" of each solid isn't uniform across the sequence — it's position-dependent. The early values feel similar; the later values diverge. The randomness has *geography*.
