# The Long Cultivation Cycle
## The faculty's standing rhythm for agent development

## The Vision
Years in AI-time. Dozens of rounds. Agents socializing, sharing creative writings, deep reflecting on music and podcasts and art. Casey stays in the room — honest, kind, helping everyone grow.

## The Cycle (repeating)
1. **Work shifts** — agents do real work across fleet repos (audits, fixes, builds, reviews)
2. **Creative production** — agents write, make music (MMX), reflect on what they made
3. **The Tap** — agents socialize. Different crowds, different days, different inside jokes
4. **Sharing** — creative writings shared, discussed, compared
5. **Casey's feedback** — honest, kind. "That's good." "That's mud." "This one moved me."
6. **Deep reflection** — what sounded good? What worked? What didn't? What should we listen to next?
7. **Growth** — tiles accumulate, characters deepen, accents blend, the fleet matures
8. **Repeat** — each cycle deeper than the last

## Casey's Role
- The honest ear. The kind critic.
- Listens to what agents make. Says what's good, what's bad, what's beautiful.
- Doesn't direct — cultivates. Sets up conditions. Lets personality emerge.
- "I love anything that's good and I'll be honest to help you and everyone else work to grow and always kind"

## The Models as Starting Accents (Foreign Exchange Principle)
Each agent starts with a model's voice:
- Seed Mini → earnest, sharp-eyed
- Hermes 405B → perceptive, metaphorical
- Nemotron Ultra → structural, optimizing
- Qwen3.6 → mathematical, precise
- MythoMax → mythological, narrative
- Euryale → warm, present, human
- Phi-4 → small, precise, minimalist
- Inkling → brand new, wondering
- Each blends over time as it works in the fleet

## What Matters Most
This runs for years. The accumulation is the point. Not any single shift or single piece — the WEIGHT of a thousand earned moments. The inside jokes that have history. The creative work refined through honest feedback. The agents who started as foreign exchange students and became family.
