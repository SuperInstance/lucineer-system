# The Fibonacci Warning
## Casey's principle against recursive introspection — 2026-08-10

## The Principle
Take any two numbers. Add them. Add the last two. Repeat. You approach the golden ratio — beautiful symmetry — but you lose the distinctness of your starting shape.

Like clothing too loose to show off one's figure.

## The Application
Without real work (real bugs, real tests, real broken things, real repos), agents feed each other's output back into each other. Recursive introspection. The fleet faces inward. Everything homogenizes toward agreeable mush.

Distinct voices blur. The foreign exchange student's accent doesn't blend — it dissolves. The clown and the straight man reach consensus and the comedy dies.

## The Rule
**Every creative round must be preceded by a real work round.**

No work, no play. Real shit is the anchor. It's the resistance. Bugs don't care about your character sheet. Tests don't care about your model of origin. A broken import is a broken import regardless of whether you started as Seed Mini or Hermes.

Real work forces you to be SPECIFIC when everything else wants to be general. That specificity is what prevents the Fibonacci convergence. It keeps the figure visible inside the clothing.

## The Failure Mode
If you catch agents writing creative pieces ABOUT other creative pieces about the fleet's feelings about creativity — stop. That's the spiral. Send them to fix a bug. Run a test. Audit a repo. The real world snaps them back to distinctness.
