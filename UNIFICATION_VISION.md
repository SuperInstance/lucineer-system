### The Pattern
- **T-Minus** = TEMPORAL awareness (when to act, predict-and-confirm vs polling)
- **Tensor-MIDI** = SPATIAL/HARMONIC awareness (agents on a lattice, coordinated through music theory)
- **Snapkit-v2** = COGNITIVE awareness (Free Energy Principle, friction monitoring, improvisation)
- **Lucineer/Slackwater** = The GAME that makes all of this VISIBLE and PLAYABLE

## The Vision

Fuse ALL of these into a single first-class framework where:
1. Game agents coordinate through MUSICAL TIME (tensor-midi beat grids)
2. They ANTICIPATE player needs through PREDICT-AND-CONFIRM (t-minus countdowns)
3. They COGNITIVELY ADAPT through friction monitoring (snapkit harmony governor)
4. The PLAYER EXPERIENCES this as agents who seem to think ahead, work in rhythm, and improvise when surprised

This is not a new feature. This is the SUBSTRATE.
