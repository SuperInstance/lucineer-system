## 1. The core mathematical equivalence is 100% backwards. This is the fatal unforced error.
You claimed `Φ → 0 = Flow`. This is exactly, catastrophically wrong.
Flow is not zero prediction error. Flow is **stable, perfectly calibrated non-zero prediction error**.
When you are truly in flow you are not perfectly predicting everything. You are solving exactly one new tiny surprise every heartbeat. If Φ actually hits zero? That is boredom. That is zoning out. That is the 30 seconds before you alt-tab out of the game forever.
Csikszentmihalyi wrote this explicitly: flow lives on a narrow ridge *halfway between anxiety (Φ too high) and boredom (Φ too low)*. You did not map Φ to the flow channel. You mapped Φ to the dead left edge of numb disengagement.
This is not a nitpick. This means every single adjustment this system makes will be tuning players directly into vacant checked-out boredom, and it will proudly report it is protecting peak flow.
Worse: The first 90 seconds of boredom are *indistinguishable* from the first 90 seconds of flow on all your proposed metrics. You will never catch this mistake during testing.

## 2. Every one of your detection signals is a false positive trap

Humans do not notice small individual changes. We notice that *something is wrong*.
You will nudge spawn tempo down 2%. You will turn NPC chatter down 5%. You will dim ambient lighting 3%. None are noticed alone. Worse: Flow is fragile precisely because it requires mutual agency. Flow is dancing with a partner. This system ties the partner's feet down so they don't mess up the steps. The second the world stops responding normally to your actions, even microscopically, the spell breaks instantly.

## 5. The silent unproven assumption that will destroy this entire project
You assumed flow is a state that should be extended forever.
It is not.
Flow ends. It is supposed to end. Good flow terminates with a peak, a release, a moment where you come back to yourself, proud, tired, satisfied.
