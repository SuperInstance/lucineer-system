# The Seven Dials — microcopy drafted by qwen3:8b (local)

**Mood** Set the sea's temperament—calm or stormy, with a flick of the wheel.
**Volume** Gauge the roar of the waves—muted or thunderous, like a ship's horn in the dark.
**Earnestness** Chart the depth of sincerity—compass needle pointing true or drifting with the tide.
**Cynicism** Calibrate the lookout's eye—sharp as a weather vane or dulled by the fog's embrace.
**Joke_landing** Tweak the punchline's anchor—secure it tight or let it drift on the current's laugh.
**Panic** Measure the squall's grip—tight as a storm's breath or loose as a loose mooring line.
**Presence** Feel the hull's weight—anchored in the room or floating on the edge of the tide.
