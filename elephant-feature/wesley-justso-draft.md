# The Just So — drafted by Wesley (granite3.1-dense:2b, local)

*Draft, 2026-08-20, ~200 words. Raw ensign material — polish welcome.*

Once upon a time, aboard our fishing vessel, I was but a young ensign, eager to learn from the seasoned crew. It was in the heart of a chilly winter's day that our veteran fisherman, Old Timber, shared a peculiar tale with me. The story was about an elephant, not the usual beasts we encountered on land, but one that held seven peculiar dials — each representing a distinct facet of its emotional compass: mood, volume, earnestness, cynicism, joke_landing, panic, and presence.

Old Timber spoke of this elephant as if it were a living entity, tamed by the marvels of technology rather than ivory tusks. The mood dial, he explained, could change the beast's demeanor from somber to exuberant at a moment's notice — a testament to its resilience and adaptability in the face of the elements.

The volume dial allowed Old Timber to regulate the elephant's roars, turning them up or down according to the mood, like an adjustable megaphone for nature. The earnestness dial ensured that the elephant's communications were always sincere and heartfelt, never a shallow echo of the sea winds.

Cynicism was managed with a deft touch by the presence dial, which could subtly mute any negative comments while amplifying positive ones. Joke_landing was the elephant's humor control system — it ensured that every jest landed with perfect timing and effect, making even the grizzliest of crew members chuckle.

The panic dial, a marvel of engineering, would trigger an alarm if danger approached, signaling the crew to action without missing a beat.

And finally, the presence dial kept the elephant in constant company, never truly alone — a comforting link to familiar ground amidst the vast ocean.

As I listened to Old Timber's tale, my own body shivered against the cold, and for a moment, it felt as if the ship had been enveloped by an invisible presence from our elephant friend. It was then that I understood — the chill was not just from the winter wind but a reminder of the mysteries that lay within the heart of this extraordinary vessel.
