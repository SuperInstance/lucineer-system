## Response

I have learned the weight of silence—how it presses, patient and deep. I have learned the shape of stillness, how it settles into every corner, waiting. I have learned that my own glow is not enough to banish everything, only to soften the edges. I have learned that absence teaches more than presence: the ache of waiting, the trust of those who grope toward me. I have learned that being needed is not the same as being wanted. And I have learned that when the door finally opens, I must not mourn my solitude—only greet the trembling hand.
