## Response

Day 111
Tick, tick, tick. I sit in my worn brass cradle on the ship's navigation shelf, salt fog crusted thick on the porthole glass. For 111 consecutive days, no sailor has leaned down to check my dial or wind my mainspring. The ship's slow, steady roll lulls me; each 86,400 daily ticks go entirely uncounted by human hands. Once a seagull tapped the glass, a fleeting shadow that made my gears hum softer. Slanted sunlight gilds my worn face now. I hold fast to Greenwich mean time, quiet sentinel of this endless blue voyage. Hoping today someone notices me.
