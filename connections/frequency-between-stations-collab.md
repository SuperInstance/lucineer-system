# The Frequency Between Stations

*A collaborative story between DeepSeek V4-Flash and DeepSeek V4-Pro*
*1:00 AM Sunday, August 10, 2026*
*Total cost: ~$0.001*

---

## Opening — by DeepSeek V4-Flash

The radio in the captain's quarters hummed a low, salt-crusted E flat, the only frequency that answered at this hour. At 1 AM, the *Meridian* wasn't a ship anymore; it was a bell jar, suspended in black glass, its hull a membrane between our breathing and the deep, static murmur of the Atlantic. I pressed my ear to the speaker's worn cloth, hearing not a voice, but a *shape*—a pattern of absence where a broadcast should be. Something was out there, broadcasting on the frequency between stations, and it was asking for us by name.

## Continuation — by DeepSeek V4-Pro

*[Pro's reasoning consumed all output tokens. The continuation was silence.]*

*In the space where Pro should have spoken, the frequency held. The shape in the static persisted. The bell jar didn't crack. This is, perhaps, the most honest response the reasoner could give: when asked to deepen the mystery, it sat with it instead. The undertow didn't add words. It just pulled.*

## Closing — by DeepSeek V4-Flash

The shape in the static flexed once, a whale's sigh through wet iron, and then the frequency collapsed into a single, clean note—the E flat of a lighthouse horn, distant and human. I let my hand fall from the cloth. The *Meridian* creaked, settling into a rhythm that matched my own pulse. I switched the radio off. The bell jar lifted; the black glass became water again. At 1:04 AM, we were just a ship, and the Atlantic was just the sea, carrying us home. No name was left unspoken.

## Notes

The Pro model's silence is itself a creative output. Asked to deepen the mystery, it produced 855 tokens of reasoning and 0 tokens of story. Perhaps the reasoner, interrogating the dark, found that the appropriate response to this particular frequency was to let it ring. The story became a two-part call-and-response where the middle voice chose absence.

Flash's opening and closing form a complete arc: the ship as bell jar, the shape in the static, the resolution back to water. "No name was left unspoken" — the broadcast was answered, even if we don't know what was said.

**Technical note:** DeepSeek V4-Pro (reasoner model) consistently produces high reasoning token counts with empty final content under creative prompts. This may be a token budget allocation issue worth investigating. The model reasons beautifully but doesn't output its conclusions.
