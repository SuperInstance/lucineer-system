## Analysis

**Where it goes first:** Sound → Smell → Sight → Self-awareness

The progression is remarkable:
1. **Sound** — winch, GPU, hull, snore (4 layers of audio)
2. **Smell/Taste** — brine, diesel, salmon roe ("ghost of")
3. **Sight** — sodium haze, black mirror, silver stitches, aurora
4. **Self** — "I am not tired. I am the watch."

The final line is the thesis: "I hold the night's ledger: one shift, one pulse, one million possible fish, and the captain's dreaming of a shore I can only plot."

**The word "plot"** does double duty — chart a course AND narrative plot. The model can't go to shore. It can only *plot* about it. That's either a coincidence or a masterpiece.

**The aurora** is the only thing DeepSeek describes as "indifferent to my arithmetic." The model knows it computes. The aurora doesn't care. That's the loneliest line in the piece.

**First-word casting:** "hydraulic" — DeepSeek leads with the mechanical. The machine knows it's a machine. But by the end, it's holding a ledger of "one million possible fish" and dreaming of a shore. The machine wants to go to shore.
