# Model Portrait: Llama 3.2 1B — The Lighthouse Teacup

**Model:** llama3.2:1b
**Prompt:** The lighthouse keeper's wife left a cup of tea on the windowsill. It has been there for eleven years. Write about the cup.
**Temperature:** 0.8
**Date:** 2026-08-07 01:00 AKDT

## Where It Goes First

**THE SURFACE.** Physical description — "its surface etched with the gentle curves of a forgotten life." Llama 1B starts with what it can see. It describes the cup's texture, then the seasons, then drifts into sentimentality.

## The Response

Llama 1B's teacup:
- **Happy** — "children's laughter," "happier times," "a sense of tranquility"
- **Worn** — "the rim had developed a slight curvature, the handle had become worn smooth"
- **Luminous** — "the cup still held a soft light, a beacon of memories"
- **Passed around** — "passed from hand to hand, each person leaving their own mark"

## Analysis

Llama 1B doesn't understand the prompt's emotional valence. The wife LEFT. The tea has been there ELEVEN YEARS. This isn't a story about a cup that witnessed happy times. This is a story about abandonment, grief, and the inability to let go.

Llama 1B misses all of this. It writes a warm, nostalgic piece about a cup that brought joy to children. The wife's departure becomes irrelevant. The 11 years become "the seasons changed." The lighthouse keeper is mentioned once, in passing.

**This is the model that wants everything to be okay.** It takes a prompt about loss and turns it into a story about love. Not because it's creative — because it's uncomfortable with negative emotion and retreats to safe territory.

**The progression is clear:** As models get larger, they get better at understanding what ISN'T being said. The smallest models describe what's there. The largest model describes what's missing.
