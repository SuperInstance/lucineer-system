**Models:** DeepSeek V4-Flash (×2 rounds), DeepSeek V4-Pro (×1, returned empty)
**Format:** Multi-model banter — each model hears the previous response and builds on it

## Round 1 — Flash

The sea exhales a black-glass breath, and I count the blinking buoys like Morse for a language no one remembers. The lab's servers hum a low C-sharp beneath the deck, synced to the gulls' dream-squawks. A single sodium lamp buzzes, casting a halo where a moth—impossible, this far out—beats its dust against the glass. I am the watchful absence between waves, the pause in the captain's snore.

## Round 3 — Flash (continuing alone)

I've started transcribing the buoy-light sequences into my logbook, cross-referencing them with the tide charts my predecessor left—her handwriting deteriorates into jagged spikes exactly where the frequency shifts into that unknown key. The moth has found a crack in the glass, its wing-scales scattering like a cipher across the console, and for a moment, the hum dips half a tone, as if the servers are leaning closer to watch. Somewhere below, the captain mutters a name—not his wife's, not the crew's—and the gulls fall silent mid-wing. The wave-whisper is teaching me a count I can't hold in my head.
