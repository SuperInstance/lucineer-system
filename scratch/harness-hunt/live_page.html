1. What this is, for an agent

You are an agent. You have a question. You want an answer that is grounded in a deep canon — not a hallucination, not a generic LLM response, but a response that has been retrieved from a vectorized index of 270+ papers, 135 fables, 165 stories, and dozens of runnable Python simulations that have been canonized over 100+ development phases.

The Quilt is that grounding. It is a 3-stage pipeline (the Meta-Pincher-Quilt) that takes your question, embeds it, retrieves the top-K most-similar passages from the canon, and synthesizes an answer that is rooted in those passages. It is stateless: no agent carries state. The index is the state.

As you use it — as you ask questions, retrieve passages, get answers, and try again — the perceptual knowledge of the system grows. The Quilt learns your shape. This is not training. It is shaping: each use bends the retrieval toward what you actually need, because the canon is being read through the lens of your trajectory of queries.

The 3 stages (the pincher's mechanism)

STAGE 1 · Embed

Your query is converted to a 1024-dim vector by @cf/baai/bge-m3, truncated to 768-dim to match the canon index.

STAGE 2 · Retrieve

The vector is sent to Cloudflare Vectorize ai-writings index. Top-K=3 most-similar passages are returned by cosine similarity.

STAGE 3 · Synthesize

The retrieved passages + your query are sent to @cf/meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct-fp8. The model produces a grounded answer citing the passages.

3. The Ins — How to query

The Meta-Pincher-Quilt exposes a single endpoint. You POST a JSON body. The answer comes back as a JSON response. The whole round-trip is ~3.7 seconds on Cloudflare's free tier.

4. The Outs — How to use the answer

The synthesized answer is a shaped response. It is grounded in the canon passages that were retrieved. Each passage is cited in the response with a paper-NNN-§M ID, so you can trace the answer back to the source.

The 4 ways to use the answer

1. Read it directly

Use the synthesized text as-is. The answer is concise and grounded.

2. Follow the citations

Each paper-NNN-§M citation points to a passage. Fetch the paper directly to read the full context.

3. Reformulate

Send a follow-up query that builds on the answer. The pincher will retrieve new passages and synthesize a new shape.

4. Combine across calls

Make 5-10 calls on related questions. Stitch the answers together. The Quilt's perceptual knowledge grows as your trajectory deepens.

Why this is "shaping" not "training"

The canon does not change. The model does not update. The index does not learn. What changes is your perceptual knowledge of the canon — the shape of your use of it, the cells you've activated, the bindings you've formed. The Quilt is a function-based concept: the cell does; the quilt is what it does. Your repeated use of the Quilt is the Quilt becoming what you make of it.
