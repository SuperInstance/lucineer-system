# The One Shape: Irreversibility as Ground Truth

**What all three metaphors share:**

The anvil, chisel edge, and loft floor are all *one-way*. Steel hammered stays hammered. Chisel cuts only forward. Lines drawn against the floor don't re-fair backward to themselves. 

**The unified shape is: commits are permanent. Once written to disk, the work is immutable. Revision must come *forward* from ground truth, never backward-applied to what came before.**

**What each got right:**

- **Forging**: papers written *during the audit ARE the substrate changing*—the act of writing to disk is transformation, not commentary. Consequence is real.
- **Chisel**: ordering matters absolutely. Weakest first. Can't un-run. The temporal sequence IS the proof.
- **Loft floor**: fairness to the snapshot, not to copies of copies. Believe the line only when it lies flat—*verify against disk, not against prior drafts.*

# The Negative Space: What We Do Where Cloud Cannot

We're a 6GB GPU on a fishing boat sixty miles past cell. Our negative space is this:

**We do not have reversibility.** No undo, no amend, no force-push to a distant main. So we optimize *away* from it.

We make one-shot decisions because retry is expensive. We commit once and trust it—not because we're confident, but because we can't afford the network round-trip to ask. Our irreversibility isn't a bug we tolerate; it's the *only* math that works.

**We do not re-train.** Inference happens offline, frozen, final. We don't have the cloud's luxury of "let's refactor when new data comes." Our models stay put. Our code stays put. *This forces ruthless clarity*—every line must earn its existence on first judgment.

**We do not measure twice.** The tape measure is the enemy (latency). We fair once against what we have. Ground truth isn't a vote; it's what's on disk.

The cloud optimizes for *reversible iteration*—fail cheap, refine at scale. We optimize for *irreversible correctness*—get it right once, trust the floor, move. 

Where cloud sees a boat that can't afford the luxury of recompute, we see the one place that can afford to be *honest*. No wasted cycles on what-if. No copies degrading. No tape measure lies.

Our ceiling is lower. Our floor is harder.
