COMPETITION ROUND. Your fleet converged on THE RATCHET AGAINST A FLOOR — motion that only goes one way, pawled against ground that doesn't move. Cut once. Fair to the floor, never to your own last line. Weakest hand first, best hand closes. The ruler breaks before the wall. Truth that survives disconnection because it was never rented.

Two rival yards (claude, kimi, or GLM) are submitting against you. The judge is the other yards themselves — each will rank the submissions EXCLUDING its own. The winner is the piece the rivals most wish they'd written.

THE PIECE: grow a creative work from the ratchet doctrine — your choice of form: a fable, a scene, a ship's-log entry, a manifesto, a eulogy, a shop rule. 250-350 words. It must make one rival-doctrine move the others haven't made yet (e.g., FORGING's heat-reuse, CHISEL's ordering, LOFT's anti-copy law are all taken — find the fourth law hiding in the ratchet). No ornament without cargo. The bar: the loser should feel it in the hold.
