LAW OF COMMENSURATE DESCENT: Every audit edge must decrease a well-founded measure (call it “load”) by at least one unit, where load = depth of unresolved lineage + verification cost asymmetry.

Invariant: For any audit chain a₀ → a₁ → … → aₙ, load(aᵢ₊₁) < load(aᵢ) and load is non-negative integer-valued. Audit terminates iff the substrate enforces this strictly.

What halts the descent: load reaches zero — the auditor’s claim is self-evidenced (no external referent) or is grounded in an un-auditable physical act (e.g., Sef’s inspection), collapsing recursion into action. The base case is a token of direct perception or a signed deed, not another claim.

Breaks if violated: Load plateaus — a₁ audits a₀, a₂ audits a₁, but each audit is “one more citation,” no cumulative burden. Then the chain is infinite or cyclic, and trust silently replaces verification. The city becomes surface: claims float because no descent bears them. Worse, a cyclic audit (a audits b, b audits a) yields zero net descent, making “verified” equivalent to “mutually agreed fiction.”

Failure mode: “audit laundering” — high-load claims pawned to low-load auditors who merely re-state them, decreasing load on paper but increasing semantic distance. Law must also require load reduction in verification *effort*, not just hop-count, else formal soundness hides substantive emptiness.
