### 2026-09-28 — zigzag-RLE steganography: the compressor's metadata IS the covert channel

**Repos:** ternary-zigzag, ternary-runlength, ternary-steganography.

Novel composition: a compression pipeline that is its own stegosystem — cover text is the weight matrix itself, and the hidden channel costs zero bytes by construction. ### 2026-09-29 (2) — rolling stego: the aggregation window IS the carrier, spread spectrum IS graceful degradation
**Direction:** fresh — the covert-channel stack moved onto the *streaming/aggregation* substrate, crossed with three-tier health routing (map line 2 on a new substrate, hybridized with line 3).

**Repos:** ternary-steganography, ternary-streaming, ternary-route, ternary-viterbi.

**Shared primitive:** the fixed-size window of trits. ternary-streaming's `StreamWindow` / `StreamSampler` fold an unbounded trit stream into majority/sum per group with O(1) memory per item; ternary-steganography's spread-spectrum mode hides one bit per *chip block* by multiplying a keyed pseudo-random ternary pattern by ±1 and recovering it by dot-product correlation. Same shape of object — a block of trits reduced to one decision — opposite intent: one for compression/aggregation, one for concealment. Nobody noticed they are the same container.

**Proposal:** make the streaming layer's own windows the stego carrier, so a payload rides a live sensor/route/decision stream with zero added bytes and no statistical trace. Today ternary-steganography's bit embedding is flagrantly detectable: it eliminates all Zeros in the encoded region (`Pos`=1, everything else=0), and its own `CapacityAnalyzer::detect_anomaly` catches that instantly. Spread spectrum fixes the statistics but costs 8 trits/bit and needs the whole chip sequence intact — one dropped sample desynchronizes everything. Composing them through ternary-streaming dissolves both limits: downsample the carrier by the chip factor *first* (`SampleMethod::Majority`), then each downsampled trit is one chip whose block-internal statistics still look natural, because the payload lives in the *sign of the sum*, not in any individual trit. Critically, `StreamAggregator` is documented as mergeable (`agg.merge(&other)`), and correlation is linear — so the decoder is a mergeable fold: two partial decoders over disjoint stream segments combine into the same payload, meaning a receiver can begin decoding mid-stream with no header and a relay can verify without the key. Then ternary-route's three-tier health becomes the degradation channel: −1 nodes contribute no chips, 0 (degraded) nodes contribute chips at reduced weight, +1 nodes at full weight. Chip loss is no longer a desync event but a weighted erasure pattern — and because spread-spectrum bits are ±1 antipodal, the surviving chips are exactly a *soft* ternary observation, which is the native input alphabet of ternary-viterbi. So the decoder becomes: correlation → per-bit soft evidence → trellis path. Graceful degradation falls out of the modulation scheme for free. Novel composition: a covert channel that *is* the sensor aggregation pipeline, whose erasure profile is the fleet's health map, decoded by a Viterbi pass the steganography builder never intended and the streaming builder never imagined carrying data.

**Falsifiable claim:** on a 3-trit carrier with chip factor c quenched by `SampleMethod::Majority` downsampling, payload recovery is exact (BER = 0) while the carrier's trit-value histogram stays within 0.02 total-variation of the un-embedded stream; under random loss of up to c−1 of every c raw trits, the `StreamAggregator`-mergeable correlation decoder retains BER ≤ 0.05 where the same payload under plain `BitEmbedder` and under stock `SpreadSpectrum` both exceed BER 0.4 — and the merged-decoder result equals the single-pass result bit-for-bit for any partition of the stream.
